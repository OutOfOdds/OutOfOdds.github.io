How a round goes

  1. 01The Oracle knows the wordOne player sees the password and can only answer “yes”, “no” or “I don’t know”.
  2. 02Beat the clockEveryone asks questions. The Mole knows the word too and helps — without getting caught.
  3. 03Find the MoleWho caught on suspiciously quickly? Talk it over and vote. Points add up across rounds.

What’s new · 1.1

  • Replay round. Someone saw the password? Deal the round again — new password, new Mole, scores stay.
  • Match score shows up before every round while the phone is passed on.
  • Clearer rules. A password is an animal, an object, a place or a job — never an action or a quality.
