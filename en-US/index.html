A word party game for 4–8 friends sharing one phone. Core gameplay works offline and requires no game account.

How to play

  1. Choose 4–8 players, enter their names and select 1–10 minutes per round.
  2. Pass the phone around so everyone can view their role privately. The Oracle and the Mole know the password; the other players do not.
  3. Ask questions aloud. The Oracle answers only yes, no or I don’t know. The Mole helps the group guess while hiding their role.
  4. When the password is guessed, record the first correct guesser, discuss the clues and vote for the Mole. If time runs out, continue to the round results.
  5. Continue until everyone has been the Oracle once. The highest total score wins.

Oracle Premium

Premium is an optional one-time purchase, not a subscription. It removes ads and unlocks creating and using custom word decks. A deck needs at least 8 unique words.

Restore a purchase

Connect to the internet, use the Apple Account that made the purchase, open Premium from Settings and choose Restore purchase. If Premium is not restored, check the connection and the purchasing account, then try again.

Restoring Premium restores the purchase entitlement. It does not recover deleted custom decks.

Offline play and ads

The first full match has no interstitial ads. In later matches, users without Premium may see an ad after even-numbered rounds and after the final round. Purchases and restoration require an internet connection; core gameplay does not.

Your words and settings

Player names, preferences and custom decks are kept on the device. Avoid deleting the app as a troubleshooting step if you need to keep your custom decks. Device backups are managed through your Apple settings.
