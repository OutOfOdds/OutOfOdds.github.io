Made for the host

Five things that stay true at every game.

Only you need the app

Players get their results, transfers and reminders as ordinary messages.

No account, no signal

No sign-up. Everything works offline, at the table or in a basement.

Your games stay yours

Nothing is stored on our servers. A session moves to another phone as one file.

Your currency

Every amount uses the currency of your iPhone's region.

No money moves through it

It's a notebook for your game. Players pay each other in cash or by bank, as always.

Questions hosts ask

Short answers. Each one links to the page with the whole story.

All 25 questions →
Why does someone pay a player they didn't play against?

Settle Up balances money, not hands. It works out the fewest transfers that leave everyone even, so any loser can pay any winner.

How Settle Up works →
Why does a player who lost get money back?

Because they paid up front. A player who bought in for $200 and lost $122 gets the rest, $78, back from the cash box. The line under each transfer shows what it's made of.

A transfer looks wrong →
A player will pay later. What do I do?

When you seat them, pick Not Yet instead of Cash or Transfer under the buy-in. They owe the cash box until you collect, and whatever is still unpaid at the end goes into the transfers.

When a player pays later →
Can rake, tips and shared costs be counted?

Yes. Chips left on the table at the end go to rake and dealer tips, part of the rake can go back to players as rakeback, and shared costs like pizza are split between the players you pick.

Rake, tips and rakeback →
The chips don't add up at the end. What now?

Chips left over are usually rake and tips. Chips short usually mean a rebuy nobody recorded.

The chips don't add up →
Does the blind timer keep going when I close the app?

Yes. It moves to the next level on its own and sends a notification when the level changes.

Notifications and Lock Screen →
Can another host take over a running game?

Yes. In the running session, tap More, then Hand Over Session, and send the file to the new host. Your copy locks, so only one phone runs the game. If you need it again, Take Back unlocks your copy, but changes made on the other phone don't come with it.
