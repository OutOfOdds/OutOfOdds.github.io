Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

House Rules is an app for people who host poker games at home, made by an independent developer, Mike Mailyan. It keeps the records of a session: who bought in, what is in the cash box and who pays whom afterwards. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app.

The short version

  • House Rules has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and no server of its own.
  • Your clubs, members and sessions are stored on your iPhone or iPad.
  • They leave the device only through your own iCloud, or when you send a file or a message yourself.
  • The developer does not collect any data from the app.

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter (clubs, member names, sessions, buy-ins, rebuys and cash-outs, the cash box, expenses, transfers, tournaments and blind timer settings) is stored locally on your device. It is included in your device's own backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) under Apple's terms. The Live Activity for the blind timer reads the timer from the same device.

iCloud

While Sync with iCloud is on in the app's Settings, your data is synced through your private iCloud database run by Apple, so your iPhone and iPad signed in to the same Apple Account show the same clubs and sessions. Only you can access it; the developer cannot read it. Turn the sync off in Settings at any time and everything stays on the device.

Other people's names

The members you add are the people you play with. Their names and results stay in your copy of the app like everything else: House Rules does not send them to the developer or to anyone else, and does not look up or contact these people.

Money

House Rules is a record-keeping tool. It does not hold, send or receive money, and it has no payment details of yours or of your members. Transfers in the app are notes about who pays whom; the payments themselves happen outside the app.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the blind timer uses them to tell you when a level ends. They are scheduled on your device. This is the only permission House Rules asks for.

Sharing and files

When you share results, export a session, save a CSV or make a backup file, the file or message goes wherever you choose to send it. From there it is covered by the terms of the app or service you sent it to.

Children

House Rules is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

Delete a session, a member or a club in the app, or delete the app to remove everything on this device. If the sync was on, remove House Rules' data in the Settings app → your name → iCloud.

This website

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics. It remembers your choice of light or dark theme in your browser. It is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its icon font from Google Fonts; both receive your IP address when a page loads, as any web server does, and handle it under their own privacy terms.

Support email

If you write to the support address, your email address and the contents of your message are used only to answer you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact
