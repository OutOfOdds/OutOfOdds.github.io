Cash Game

Buy-Ins, Rebuys and Cash-Outs

Every chip that changes hands between you and a player goes through the same keys in House Rules:

  • a buy-in seats a player with their first chips;
  • a rebuy gives a seated player more chips;
  • a cash-out records the chips a player hands back when they leave.

From these three House Rules knows how many chips are on the table, how much money you hold, and at the end of the night who won and who lost.

Two words you'll see everywhere:

  • Chip value is what a chip is worth in money, set when you start the session, like 1 chip = $0.25. You enter chips, House Rules shows the money. See Chip Value.
  • The cash box is the money players have paid you, the host, for their chips, in cash or by transfer. It's what you'll pay winners from at the end. See The Cash Box.

The example night

All the screenshots come from one night at the Maple Street Game.

  • The game: No-Limit Hold'em, blinds 4/8, 1 chip = $0.25. The usual buy-in is 400 chips, which costs $100.
  • The host: Dave. He plays too and records everything on his phone, so in this story you are Dave.
  • Two and a half hours in: Dave, Marcus, Jenna, Tyler and Priya have bought in for 400 each, and Marcus has rebought once for another 400. That's 2,400 chips on the table, and everyone has paid, so $600 in the cash box.
  • What happens next, in this order: Sam sits down for the usual 400 and pays cash. Kevin, a friend of Jenna's, plays for the first time and pays by transfer. Tyler rebuys 400 and Priya 200. Then Dave, who bought in once for 400, leaves with 652 chips.

The bar at the bottom of the session has three buttons: Cash Box, with the money you hold, Rebuy, and + for a new player.

Seat a player

Tap + at the bottom. The New Player sheet opens with a search field, New Member and the list of Club Members who aren't at the table yet.

A club member

  1. Tap their name under Club Members.
  2. The keys open for their buy-in. Type the chips, or tap a suggestion next to the digits.
  3. Under the amount, choose how they pay: Not Yet, Cash or Transfer.
  4. Tap the checkmark on the keys.

The suggestions for a buy-in are the usual buy-in at this table (the first buy-in most players made tonight), twice it and three times it. A suggestion records the buy-in right away, with the payment you chose, so choose Cash, Transfer or Not Yet first. Above the keys, Cost shows what the chips cost in money at the session's chip value.

Someone new

Kevin has never played at the club. He isn't in the list, so Dave adds him:

  1. Tap New Member and type the name. You can also type it in the search field and tap Add with the name in quotes.
  2. Tap the arrow next to the name. The buy-in keys open.
  3. Type the chips, choose how they pay, and tap the checkmark.

Kevin becomes a member of the club, so next time he's in the list.

How they pay

  • Cash or Transfer: the player paid you now. The money goes into the cash box.
  • Not Yet: the player will pay later. They owe the cash box until you collect, see When a Player Pays Later.

Not Yet is chosen by default, unless the club's rules set paying to cash or transfer up front. Then that one is chosen. Those rules are a club setting also called House Rules, see House Rules.

After a buy-in

A short message at the bottom confirms it, like Player added with Kevin, $100 in the cash box. Tap Undo in it if you seated the wrong person.

The New Player sheet stays open, so you can seat the next player straight away. Close it with the ✕ at the top right when everyone is seated.

Record a rebuy

There are two ways. Both open the same keys.

The Rebuy button

Tap Rebuy at the bottom of the session, then the player. Here Tyler rebuys for 400 and pays cash.

The two buttons at the bottom of this sheet, Rebuy and Cash Out, switch what the list is for: pick Cash Out and a tap on a player opens their cash-out instead. When someone has cashed out, a third button, Back in Game, appears there too.

A swipe on the row

Swipe right on the player's row and tap Rebuy. Priya wants half a buy-in, so Dave chooses Cash and taps 200 half.

You can also touch and hold the row and choose Rebuy from the menu.

Cash out a player

When a player leaves, count their chips and record them. Swipe left on the row and tap the Cash Out button that appears. The keys open: type the chips, then tap Cash Out at the top right of the keys to save. (The Rebuy sheet's Cash Out list opens the same keys.)

Above the keys you see what the cash-out means before you save it:

  • Bought in: all the chips the player bought tonight;
  • Result: what they won or lost in money, here +$63 (652 chips minus 400, at $0.25 a chip);
  • Left on table: the chips that stay in play after they leave.

After the cash-out Dave moves under Cashed Out, and the table has 3,148 chips: the 3,800 bought tonight minus Dave's 652. Their worth at the chip value is $787.

The cash box stays at $950, the money paid for all 3,800 chips. A cash-out doesn't take money out of it: Dave doesn't get paid on the spot. At the end of the night House Rules works out who pays whom, and winners are paid from the cash box first, see How Settle Up Works.

More chips than on the table

While other players are still seated, a cash-out can't be more than the chips on the table. If you type too many, the keys say by how much, and Cash Out stays off. Here a slip of the finger made Marcus's 500 into 50,000:

For the last player still seated, the keys offer Whole Stack: one tap records everything left on the table as their cash-out. If they hand back a different number, record what they hand back. The last player is the one exception to the rule above: they can cash out with more chips than House Rules has on the table, which usually means a rebuy nobody recorded. The keys say how many, like 52 chips short. You can sort it out in Close Session., and Cash Out still works. You deal with the short chips when you close the session, see Close Session, Step by Step.

Bring a player back

A player who cashed out sits down again later? Tap their row under Cashed Out, then Back in Game. The keys open as for a rebuy: type the chips, choose how they pay, and tap the checkmark. The player moves back to the table.

You can also swipe right on the row under Cashed Out and tap Back in Game.

Fix a mistake

Right after an entry, tap Undo in the message at the bottom.

Later, tap the player's row. Their sheet shows what they bought in, how long they've played, and every entry with its time.

Tap an entry to change its amount, or touch and hold it for Edit Amount and Delete Entry. A new amount changes the chips only. The money the player paid into the cash box for that entry stays as it was, so if they paid more or less than you first recorded, fix the payment in the Cash Box too.

Delete Player at the bottom removes the player and all their chip entries from the session. It only works while none of the session's money is tied to the player. Otherwise House Rules says what to clear first:

  • This player has cash box payments. Delete them first. A buy-in paid by cash or transfer counts: delete the player's lines in the Cash Box.
  • This player paid for or shares an expense. Remove them from expenses first.
  • This player has completed transfers. Unmark them first.

Both deletes ask you to confirm first. After a change or a delete, the chips on the table and the player's result are counted again.

Questions

Do I record chips or money? Chips. House Rules turns them into money with the session's chip value. More about it: Chip Value.

A player will pay at the end of the night. What do I choose? Not Yet. They owe the cash box until you collect, see When a Player Pays Later.

Why didn't the cash box go down after a cash-out? A cash-out records the chips a player hands back. Who gets paid how much is worked out once, at the end of the night, in Close Session.

Can a player cash out more chips than they bought? Yes, that's a win. They just can't cash out more chips than are on the table.

Is there a limit on rebuys? No. Record as many as the player makes.

Can I change the chip value in the middle of the game? Yes, with Edit Session in the session's More menu. See Chip Value.
