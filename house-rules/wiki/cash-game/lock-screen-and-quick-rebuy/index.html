Cash Game

Lock Screen and Quick Rebuy

While a cash game runs, House Rules puts a card on your iPhone's Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, the black pill at the top of the screen. The card shows the table at a glance, and its + button takes you straight to Rebuy. You don't have to unlock, find the app and open the session when someone wants more chips.

iOS calls cards like this Live Activities.

The example

It's two and a half hours into a night at the Maple Street Game: five players and 2,400 chips on the table. Each chip is worth $0.25, so 400 chips cost $100. Dave, the host, has put his phone down.

Marcus has already bought chips twice tonight: 400 to sit down and 400 more at 11:15 PM. Buying more chips during the game is a rebuy. Now he has lost again and wants another 400, paid in cash. The cash goes into the cash box, the money players have paid Dave for their chips (see The Cash Box).

Where the card appears

The card appears on its own once a cash game is running and you've opened the app. There's nothing to turn on.

  • Dynamic Island (on iPhones that have one). Outside the app, the island shows a small dot on the left and how long the session has run on the right, in hours and minutes. Tap the island to open the session.
  • Lock Screen. The full card, at the bottom of the Lock Screen. You also see it when you swipe down from the top left of the screen to open Notification Center.

The first time, iOS asks under the card: Allow Live Activities from House Rules? Tap Allow to keep the card. With Don't Allow the card goes away, and you can turn it back on later in the iPhone Settings app.

What the card shows

From top to bottom:

  • The club and the clock. The dot in the club's color, Maple Street Game, and how long the session has run: 2 hours 30 minutes 36 seconds. It keeps counting on its own.
  • At the table: how many players are still in out of everyone who played tonight. 5 of 5 means nobody has cashed out.
  • On the table: the chips in play, 2,400.
  • The + button, round, on the right. It opens Rebuy.
  • Last: the latest entry, with its chips and time. Here it's Marcus's earlier rebuy of 400 at 11:15 PM.

Tap anywhere on the card except + to open the session.

Add a rebuy from the card

  1. On the Lock Screen, tap + on the card. If the phone is locked, iOS may ask you to unlock it first.
  2. House Rules opens on Rebuy, with everyone at the table. Tap the player.
  3. Above the digits, choose how they pay: Cash or Transfer if they pay you now, Not Yet if they'll pay later.
  4. Type the chips and tap the checkmark at the top right of the keys, or tap a suggestion next to the digits. A suggestion records the rebuy at once.

The keys are the same as in the app. More about rebuys and suggestions: Buy-Ins, Rebuys and Cash-Outs. If a player pays later, see When a Player Pays Later.

The toast says Rebuy recorded, Marcus, $100 in the cash box. The session now has 2,800 chips on the table. Tapped the wrong player? Tap Undo on the toast.

The card follows: On the table is now 2,800, and Last shows the new rebuy.

When the card changes or goes away

  • When everyone has cashed out, + goes away and the card says Everyone cashed out until you close the session.
  • Closing or deleting the session removes the card. See Close Session Step by Step.
  • iOS removes Live Activities on its own after several hours. Open the app and the card comes back.
  • The card shows one cash game: the latest one running.
  • Tournaments don't get this card. They use the blind timer card instead, see Notifications and Lock Screen.

Turn it off

On the House Rules home screen, open Settings, and under Live Activities switch off Lock Screen. The same switch covers the blind timer card.

If Live Activities are turned off for House Rules in the iPhone Settings app, the House Rules Settings say so under the switch, and the card can't appear until you turn them back on there.

Questions

I don't see the card. Check three things: a cash game is running, you've opened House Rules since it started, and Lock Screen is on under Live Activities in House Rules Settings. If you tapped Don't Allow when iOS asked, turn Live Activities back on for House Rules in the iPhone Settings app.

Can I record a buy-in for a new player from the card? No, + opens Rebuy for players already at the table. For a new player, open the session and tap + at the bottom right.

Can I cash someone out from the card? Not from the card itself. Rebuy opens with a Cash Out tab at the bottom, so you can switch to it there.

My iPhone has no Dynamic Island. The card still appears on the Lock Screen and in Notification Center.

Do other players see the card? No. It's only on your phone, the one running the session.
