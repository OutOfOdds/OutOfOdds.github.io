Cash Game

When a Player Pays Later

Sometimes a player sits down without the money: no cash on them, or they'll send it later. You don't have to keep them waiting or remember it yourself. Seat them with Not Yet, and House Rules keeps track of what they owe the cash box until they pay.

The cash box is the money players have paid you, the host, for their chips. More about it: The Cash Box.

The example

It's two and a half hours into a night at the Maple Street Game.

  • The game: No-Limit Hold'em with blinds of 4 and 8 chips, 1 chip = $0.25. The usual buy-in is 400 chips, which costs $100.
  • The table: Dave, the host, with Jenna, Marcus, Priya and Tyler. Each bought 400 chips, and Marcus bought 400 more later (a rebuy). Everyone has paid: Jenna by transfer, the rest in cash. So the cash box holds $600: $500 in cash and $100 by transfer.
  • What happens: Sam arrives without cash and buys in for 400 chips, paying nothing yet. An hour later he hands Dave $60 in cash. $40 is still owed.

Seat a player who hasn't paid

  1. In the session, tap + at the bottom right and pick the player. For a player already at the table who buys more chips, tap Rebuy at the bottom instead and pick them (see Buy-Ins, Rebuys and Cash-Outs).
  2. Above the digits, choose Not Yet. It's the first of three choices: Not Yet, Cash and Transfer.
  3. Type the chips, or tap a suggestion next to the digits. A suggestion records the buy-in at once.

Not Yet is chosen for you unless your club's House Rules say players pay up front in cash or by transfer. See House Rules.

The toast says Player added with just the name. When a player pays on the spot, it also says how much went into the cash box. Here nothing did.

Where you see what's owed

Sam's row in the session says 1 buy-in, owes $100. His 400 chips cost $100 at $0.25 a chip, and he hasn't paid any of it.

Tap Cash Box at the bottom left. It still holds $600, and the tile still owed shows $100. Under Before You Settle Up there's a line Sam owes $100: he can stay unpaid until you settle up at the end of the night. Settling up is when House Rules works out who pays whom so everyone ends the night even. The line above it, 6 of 6 players at the table, only reminds you that everyone cashes out before that.

Collect when they pay

An hour later Sam hands Dave $60 in cash. To record it:

  1. In Cash Box, tap Collect at the bottom left.
  2. Under Owe the Cash Box, tap the player. Everyone who owes is listed here first, with the amount.
  3. The keys open. Tap all owed next to the digits for the whole amount, or type a part of it. Sam pays 60.
  4. Choose Cash or Transfer above the digits, the way they paid you.
  5. Check the two lines above the keys: Owes is what the player owed, Cash box after is what the cash box will hold. Then tap the checkmark at the top right of the keys, Collect Payment.

The toast says Payment collected with the name and the amount. Undo takes it back.

Now the cash box holds $660: $560 in cash and $100 by transfer. still owed is down to $40, and Sam owes $40 under Before You Settle Up. The payment is also in the list of operations further down, as Sam, payment, +$60.

If they still owe at the end

You don't have to collect everything before the night ends.

  • In Close Session, the Collect step lists who still owes. Collect there, or tap Continue to skip it. See Close Session Step by Step.
  • Whatever is still unpaid goes into the transfers, the payments House Rules lists at settle-up (not the same as paying by Transfer). Sam's $40 is counted in what he pays or gets then. See How Settle Up Works.
  • A transfer nobody marks as done stays in Who Owes on the club screen, so it isn't forgotten after tonight. See Who Owes and Reminders.
check_circle

Not Yet doesn't change anyone's result. Results come from chips: Sam's win or loss is counted the same whether he paid or not. Only the money he still owes changes.

Questions

Can a rebuy be unpaid too? Yes. The rebuy keys have the same Not Yet, Cash and Transfer choices.

The player paid the whole amount. What do I tap? In Collect, pick them and tap all owed next to the digits. It's the full amount they owe.

Sam wins chips later. Does he owe less? No. He owes what his chips cost when he bought them, $100. Winning or losing changes his result, and settle-up puts the two together at the end.

Can they pay part in cash and part by transfer? Yes. Collect twice: once with Cash, once with Transfer.

I chose Not Yet by mistake, they did pay. Collect the payment right away with all owed.

Does the cash box total include what's owed? No. In Cash Box is only the money you actually got. What's owed is shown apart, as still owed.
