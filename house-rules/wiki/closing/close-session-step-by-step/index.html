Closing and Settling Up

Close Session, Step by Step

When the game is over, a few things are left before everyone goes home: the last players cash out, the leftover chips get assigned, the pizza gets split, and the money changes hands. Close Session walks you through all of it, one step at a time, so nothing is forgotten.

This article goes through every step on one night. For a shorter first run, see Your First Cash Game.

A few words used below:

  • Cash box: the money players have paid you, the host, for their chips. See The Cash Box.
  • Result: what a player won or lost tonight, in money.
  • Settle up: House Rules works out who pays whom, so everyone ends the night even. The payments it lists are called transfers.

The example

It's late at the Maple Street Game.

  • The game: No-Limit Hold'em, 1 chip = $0.25.
  • The table: six players bought 3,200 chips in all, at $100 for 400 chips. Everyone except Tyler has cashed out:
    • Jenna bought 400, cashed out 1,108: won $177. She paid by transfer.
    • Dave, the host, bought 400, cashed out 652: won $63.
    • Priya bought 400, cashed out 548: won $37.
    • Sam bought 400 without paying, cashed out 224: lost $44.
    • Marcus bought 800, cashed out 236: lost $141.
    • Tyler bought 800 and is still playing.
  • The cash box: $700, $600 in cash and Jenna's $100 by transfer.
  • Still to do:
    • Tyler is the last at the table, with 312 chips.
    • Once he cashes out, 120 chips are left on the table: 80 are the house's rake, 40 are tips for the dealer.
    • Dave paid $48 for pizza that everyone ate. It's recorded but not split yet.
    • Sam sat down without paying for his chips and still owes the cash box.

Open Close Session

  • Once everyone has cashed out, Close Session is the big button at the bottom of the session screen, in place of Rebuy.
  • While someone is still at the table, tap More (the three dots at the top right) and choose Close Session. Here Tyler is still in, so Dave uses the menu.

The five steps

Close Session lists five steps, always in this order:

  1. Cash Out: everyone leaves the table.
  2. Rake and Tips: the chips left on the table are assigned.
  3. Expenses: every expense is split.
  4. Collect: money players still owe the cash box.
  5. Settle Up: who pays whom.

Each step shows a number while it's waiting and a checkmark when it's done. Under its name, a line says what's left, like 1 of 6 players at the table. A step you don't need says Not needed.

The current step, the first one not done, is the bright one. The big button at the bottom always does the current step, and its name changes with it: Cash Out, Rake and Tips, Split Expense, Continue, and at the end Finish Session. You can also tap a step's row to open it.

Step 1. Cash Out

Tap Cash Out at the bottom. The players still at the table are listed. Pick the player, type the chips they hand back and tap the Cash Out button next to the digits to record it.

Tyler is the only one left, so he's already picked. He hands back 312 chips: he bought 800, so he lost 488 chips, which at $0.25 is $122. Above the keys you see what he Bought in, 800 chips, his Result, −$122, and what's Left on table after him, 120 chips. For the last player at the table, Whole Stack next to the digits fills in every chip still on the table, 432 here. Use it only when no chips are left for rake or tips.

More about cash-outs: Buy-Ins, Rebuys and Cash-Outs.

Step 2. Rake and Tips

Cash Out now has a checkmark: Everyone cashed out. Rake and Tips says 120 chips left over.

  1. Tap Rake and Tips at the bottom. All 120 chips start in Rake.
  2. Type the rake, 80, and tap the arrow (→) at the top right of the keys. The Tips get the rest, 40, on their own.
  3. Tap the checkmark.

If nobody takes rake or tips at your table, every chip is cashed out and this step says Not needed. If the table has fewer chips than were bought, the step asks who covers the missing chips instead, see The Chips Don't Add Up. More about rake: Rake, Tips and Rakeback.

Under the step, two more rows appear:

  • Rakeback: tap it to give part of the rake back to players. Here it's Not distributed.
  • Kept by House: the rake that stays with the house, the host's side of the game, $20 (80 chips at $0.25). The tips, $10, are the dealer's.

Step 3. Expenses

Expenses says 1 expense to split, and the card below lists it: Pizza, not split, $48.

  1. Tap Split Expense at the bottom. The Split Expense screen opens for the pizza.
  2. Under Paid From, choose Player and tap Dave. Dave is then checked under Who Shares for you.
  3. Under Who Shares, tap the other five, one by one. With Equally, the $48 is split six ways, $8 each.
  4. Tap the checkmark at the top right of the screen. You're back in Close Session.

With several expenses, the button takes you through them one by one. Each share comes off that player's result, and Dave gets the $48 back. More about splitting: Expenses.

Step 4. Collect

Expenses has a checkmark: All expenses are split. Collect says 1 player owes $52.

Why $52? Sam bought 400 chips, $100, without paying. He cashed out 224 chips, worth $56, and his pizza share is $8. So he owes $100 − $56 + $8 = $52. Before the pizza was split, the step said $44.

The cash box holds $700, but only $600 of it is cash: Jenna's $100 came by transfer. Two cards are under the steps:

  • Cash: Should be in cash is the cash House Rules expects you to hold, $600. It counts only cash, not transfers. Tap Count Cash to count your bills against it before you hand any out, see The Cash Box.
  • Owes the Cash Box: everyone who still owes, here Sam owes $52.

Sam pays his $52 in cash:

  1. Tap Sam owes. The payment keys open.
  2. Cash is chosen. Tap 52 all owed next to the digits.
  3. Tap the checkmark at the top right of the keys.

Once nobody owes, Collect gets its checkmark, Everyone has paid, and you move straight on to Settle Up.

Collect is optional. If a player can't pay tonight, tap Continue at the bottom: what they owe goes into the transfers in the next step. More about it: When a Player Pays Later.

Step 5. Settle Up

The last step lists the transfers that settle the night: Settle Up, 0 of 5 marked. Here they're all From Cash Box: Dave hands each player their money from the cash box, which now holds $752. Dave is on the list too: his $203 is the part of the box that's his, so he just keeps it.

Each transfer explains its amount. Tyler's line reads Game −$122, expense share −$8, paid in +$200: he lost $122, owes $8 for pizza and paid $200 for his chips, so he gets $70 back. The five transfers:

  • Jenna, $269: won $177, pizza −$8, paid in $100.
  • Dave, $203: won $63, gets back $48 for the pizza, pizza −$8, paid in $100.
  • Priya, $129: won $37, pizza −$8, paid in $100.
  • Tyler, $70: lost $122, pizza −$8, paid in $200.
  • Marcus, $51: lost $141, pizza −$8, paid in $200.

Together they're $722, and $30 stays in the box. Nothing for Sam: he paid what he owed in step 4. How House Rules finds these transfers: How Settle Up Works.

When money moves, tap the transfer, then Mark as Paid. The full amount is already filled in. A marked transfer gets a checkmark and is struck through. See Marking Transfers Done.

Finish or close for now

While any transfer is still open, the button at the bottom says Close for Now. Tap it and confirm: the session waits in a Needs Settling group, a list of sessions with open transfers, on the Home screen and the club screen, and the open transfers show in Who Owes (see Who Owes and Reminders). When the money comes in, open the session, tap Close Session and mark the rest.

To keep the session open and carry on marking, tap anywhere on the screen outside this box.

Once every transfer is marked, the button says Finish Session.

Tap Finish Session. The session ends and shows Session Ended with everyone's results. These include the pizza: Dave is up $40 for it, everyone else down $8. So the winners have +$301 in all, and the three losers −$331. The $30 between them is the rake and tips, $20 and $10, and it's exactly what's left in the Cash Box.

A finished session can't be changed by accident. To fix a mistake, tap the lock at the bottom right to reopen it. Transfers you already marked stay marked.

Questions

Do I have to do the steps in order? The button always does the first step that isn't done. You can tap the rows of Cash Out, Rake and Tips and Expenses to open them, but Settle Up waits until steps 1 to 3 are done and Collect is done or skipped with Continue.

Can I close the session while someone is still playing? Yes, from More, Close Session. Step 1 is then to cash them out.

A step says Not needed. There's nothing to do there tonight, like Rake and Tips when every chip was cashed out, or Expenses when there are none.

I closed for now. Where's the session? On the Home screen and the club screen, under Needs Settling. Open it and tap Close Session to carry on with the transfers.

Can I share the transfers with the players? Yes. On the Settle Up step, tap the share button at the top right. See Sharing Results.
