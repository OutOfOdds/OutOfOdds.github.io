Closing and Settling Up

Marking Transfers Done

At the end of the night, Settle Up lists the transfers: who pays whom, and how much. As money changes hands, you mark each transfer as paid. When every transfer is marked, you finish the session. Where the list comes from is in How Settle Up Works.

The example

It's the end of a night at the Maple Street Game. Dave is the host: he runs the game, holds the money and plays too. 1 chip = $0.25.

  • The cash box, the money players paid Dave for their chips: $700. Jenna paid $100 by bank transfer, the rest $600 in cash. Sam hasn't paid yet.
  • The list: seven transfers.
    • From Cash Box: Jenna $269, Dave $203, Priya $129, Tyler $70, Marcus $29. Dave hands this money out.
    • Between Players: Sam → Marcus, $22. Sam pays Marcus directly.
    • To Cash Box: Sam, $30. Sam pays Dave.
  • Where the amounts come from: each line under a name shows it, for example Jenna's Game +$177, expense share −$8, paid in +$100: she won $177, her share of the pizza is $8, and she gets back the $100 she paid for her chips. Dave paid $48 for the pizza, so his line also has paid for expenses +$48. How the list is built is in How Settle Up Works.
  • What happens: Dave sends Jenna her $269 by bank. He pays the others in cash and keeps his own $203. Sam pays Marcus $22, but has only $20 for Dave, so $10 stays open.

You find the list in the last step of Close Session, Settle Up (see Close Session, Step by Step). Each transfer has a circle on the left. 0 of 5 under a group means none of its five transfers is marked yet.

Paid by bank: Mark Paid by Transfer

The five transfers from the cash box add up to $700, but Dave holds only $600 in cash: Jenna's $100 is in his bank. If he paid everyone in cash, he'd be $100 short. So he pays Jenna, the biggest one, by bank. A cash box transfer counts as cash unless you say otherwise, so tell House Rules it went by bank. Then Count Cash, the check of the cash in your hands, expects the right amount.

  1. Touch and hold Jenna.
  2. Choose Mark Paid by Transfer.

Jenna's row is checked and crossed out, and the group says 1 of 5.

The menu has two more choices: Mark as Paid marks the full amount in cash, and Mark Partial Payment opens the keys, like a tap.

Paid in cash: tap and Mark as Paid

Dave plays too, and the cash box owes him $203 like anyone else. He takes it from the cash box himself, so it's still a transfer to mark.

  1. Tap Dave. The keys open with the full amount, $203, and Full amount under it.
  2. Tap Mark as Paid at the top right of the keys.

The keys are the number pad that opens at the bottom. The 203 All button next to the digits puts the full amount back if you've changed it.

A marked cash box transfer also records the money in the cash box: a payout to the player, or a payment from them. You don't record it twice.

Marked by mistake

Say Dave tapped his row by accident. Tap a checked transfer to take the mark off. It's open again, and its cash box entry goes with the mark.

To mark it again, Dave taps his row and Mark as Paid.

Between players: Confirmed By

Sam pays Marcus $22 directly. Dave doesn't hold that money, so the keys ask who says it was paid.

  1. Tap Sam → Marcus.
  2. Confirmed By is a menu: tap it to choose Marcus confirmed, Sam showed it or I saw it myself. It starts on the one who gets the money, Marcus confirmed. I saw it myself means you watched the money change hands.
  3. Tap Mark as Paid.

A transfer between players doesn't touch the cash box, and its menu has no Mark Paid by Transfer: how two players pay each other is up to them.

Dave pays Priya, Tyler and Marcus in cash the same way: tap, Mark as Paid. Everything but Sam's $30 is now marked.

Sam's line says owed for the session: he lost at the table and hasn't paid for his chips. The results add up to −$30, because $20 of rake and $10 of dealer tips left the game. Sam's $30 brings that money back to the cash box, where it belongs to the house and the dealer.

Part of the amount: Record Payment

Sam has only $20 for Dave.

  1. Tap Sam under To Cash Box. The keys open with $30.
  2. Delete it and type 20. Under the amount: $10 stays open. The button at the top right is now Record Payment.
  3. Tap Record Payment.

A partial payment changes what's still owed, so House Rules builds the list again. Sam now has a transfer of $10, with Paid $20 of $30 under it. Sam's $20 counts as a transfer of its own, so there are eight now: seven paid and Sam's $10. Settle Up says 7 of 8 marked.

The transfers you marked move to Already Done, with a grey check: Done before the plan was recalculated. The plan above already counts them. They're kept as a record, and tapping them does nothing. To undo one of those, delete its payment or payout in the cash box (see The Cash Box).

So record partial payments last, after the transfers you might still want to undo with a tap.

When something stays open

While a transfer is open, the button at the bottom says Close for Now. Tap it, then Close for Now again: the session waits until the last transfer is marked. The bell next to Sam sends him a reminder, see Who Owes and Reminders.

When every transfer is marked, the button becomes Finish Session. A finished session can only be read. To change it, open the session, then its settlement, and tap Reopen.

Questions

I marked the wrong transfer. Tap it again. If it's already under Already Done, delete its payment or payout in the cash box.

Should I mark my own transfer? Yes. Dave's $203 is his money in the cash box, and marking it records that he took it.

Does a transfer between players change my cash? No. That money goes from one player to another, not through you.

Where do I see what's still owed later? In the club's Who Owes, see Who Owes and Reminders.
