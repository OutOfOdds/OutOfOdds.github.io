Closing and Settling Up

Sharing Results

At the end of the night, everyone wants to know where they stand: what they won or lost, and whom they pay. House Rules writes it for you. You can send each player only their own part, share the whole list of payments in a group chat, or make a Session Report, a picture of the night.

You share from Settle Up, the last step of Close Session (see Close Session, Step by Step). A transfer is one payment from settle-up: who pays, who gets it, how much (see How Settle Up Works).

The example

It's the end of a night at the Maple Street Game. Dave is the host: he runs the game, holds the cash box, the money players paid for their chips, and plays too.

  • The game: 1 chip = $0.25. Rake was 80 chips, $20, and tips for the dealer 40 chips, $10. Dave paid $48 for pizza, shared by all six, $8 each.
  • Six players: Jenna, Dave and Priya are up. Sam, Tyler and Marcus are down. Everyone paid for their chips except Sam.
  • Seven transfers. What the cash box pays a player is what they paid for their chips plus their result: Jenna paid $100 and won $169, so she gets $269. Tyler paid $200 and lost $130, so he still gets $70 back. Sam paid nothing and lost $52, so he owes $52: $22 to Marcus and $30 to the cash box, that is to Dave.
  • Marked: Dave has already sent Jenna her $269 by bank and marked it paid, see Marking Transfers Done. The other six are open.
  • What Dave does: sends Sam his own message, shares the list in the group chat, and makes a Session Report.

The Share button

In Settle Up, tap Share at the top right, the square with an arrow. The menu opens over it with three choices:

  • Send to Each Player: a personal message for each player.
  • Share Transfers: the whole list as text.
  • Session Report: an image or text with the results and transfers.

Once the session is finished, open it from the club or the Lobby: its Settlement has the same Share button.

Send to Each Player

Tap Send to Each Player. The list, titled Send to Each, has the players in groups:

  • Pays: players who owe, with what's still open. Sam, 2 transfers, $52.
  • Receives: players who get money, like Dave, from the cash box, $203. Dave holds the cash box, so he takes his own share from it. Tyler and Marcus are down tonight but get money back, because they paid more for their chips than they lost. Jenna's is already paid, so she has All received.
  • Even: players with nothing to pay or get, if there are any.

Tap a player. The share sheet opens, the iPhone's panel for sending things: pick the app you message players with. Nothing is sent until you send it.

Each player's message has only their part:

  • Hi Sam,, the club and the date.
  • Your result, and How it adds up: the game, the expense share and what they paid in.
  • Please send: the transfers they make, like Sam's $22 to Marcus and $30 to Dave. You'll receive: the ones they get. Paid ones go under Already sent: and Already received:.
  • For a player who pays another player: Send it straight to them, not to me.
  • Thanks! and Calculated with House Rules.

If a member has a payment link saved in the club, the message to whoever pays them includes it.

Back in the list, Sam's row says Sent, and the caption at the top counts 1 of 6 sent. Sent only means you tapped the row: House Rules can't tell whether the message went out. It lasts while the list is open.

Tap ✕ at the top left to close the list.

Share Transfers

Tap Share, then Share Transfers. The share sheet opens with Session Settlement: the club and date, then every transfer, grouped like in Settle Up, with the open ones and the paid ones apart. Good for a group chat where everyone can see the whole picture.

Session Report

Tap Share, then Session Report. At the top, choose Image or Text. Under Include, choose what goes in:

  • Player Results: each player's chips, expense share and result. In is the chips they bought, in money, whether they've paid or not: Sam's in $100 is chips he still owes for. Out is what their chips were worth at the end.
  • Transfers: who pays whom, with a check next to the paid ones.
  • House Numbers: rake, tips and what the house keeps.

Expenses are always in the report. The club remembers your choice for the next time.

Below Include is the Preview, exactly what you'll send. While transfers are open, it says so at the top: Not settled yet, 6 transfers open.

House Numbers

Players may not need to see what the house took. So when you turn House Numbers on, House Rules asks first: Players will see what the house took tonight: rake, tips and what the house keeps. Tap Show House Numbers.

The report gets a House part: Rake and house fee $20 is what the house took. Kept by House $20 is what it keeps after rakeback, all of it here, since no rakeback was given. Dealer tips $10 goes to the dealer. See Rake, Tips and Rakeback.

Image or text

Image makes a picture for a chat. Text makes the same report as plain text, for a message or a note. House Numbers is still on here, so the text has the house part too, further down.

Tap the checkmark at the top right. The share sheet opens: send the report to a chat, or save the image to Photos or Files.

Questions

Will players see each other's results in Send to Each Player? No. Each message has only that player's result and transfers. Use Share Transfers or the report to show everyone everything.

Can I share before every transfer is paid? Yes. Open transfers are marked as open, and the report says Not settled yet.

How do I remind someone who still hasn't paid? Use the bell next to their transfer, or Who Owes on the club screen, see Who Owes and Reminders.

Can I send the whole session to another phone? Yes, as a file, see Export and Import.
