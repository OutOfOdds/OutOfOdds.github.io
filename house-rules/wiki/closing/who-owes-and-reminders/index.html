Closing and Settling Up

Who Owes and Reminders

Not everyone pays at the table. When you close a session with transfers still open, they don't get lost: they stay with the club, in Who Owes. There you see who still owes whom, mark payments as they come in, and send reminders.

A transfer is one payment from settle-up: who pays, who gets it, how much. How they're built is in How Settle Up Works, and how you mark them in Marking Transfers Done.

The example

It's the end of a night at the Maple Street Game. Dave is the host: he runs the game, holds the cash box, the money players paid for their chips, and plays too. In this article, "you" is Dave.

  • Paid tonight: Dave sends Jenna her $269 by bank, keeps his own $203, and pays Priya $129 and Marcus $29 in cash.
  • Still open:
    • Cash Box → Tyler, $70. The cash box owes Tyler $70, but Tyler has already gone home. The money stays in the cash box for now.
    • Sam → Marcus, $22. Sam owes Marcus.
    • Sam → Cash Box, $30. Sam owes Dave.
  • The next day: Sam pays Marcus his $22, and Dave sends Tyler $20 of the $70 he's owed.

Close the session for now

In Settle Up, the last step of Close Session, mark what's paid: a checked, crossed-out transfer is paid. Three transfers stay open, Tyler's $70 further up under From Cash Box, and Sam's two below. So the button at the bottom says Close for Now. Tap it, then Close for Now again.

The session isn't finished: it needs settling. The Lobby shows it under Needs Settling with 1 to settle at the top, and so does the club screen. $800 not settled is the session's buy-ins, $800 of chips, not what's owed.

Who Owes on the club screen

Tap the club in the Lobby, here Maple Street Game, and scroll to the Who Owes card.

  • $52 under Who Owes: what players owe in all.
  • Each player who owes, with the total: Sam, 2 transfers, oldest Nov 23, $52. The card shows up to three players.
  • You Owe, from the cash box: what the cash box still has to pay out, here Tyler's $70. That's your debt as the host, so it's apart from the players.
  • Show All opens the full list.

When nobody owes, the card isn't there.

The full list

Tap Show All. Who Owes has two tabs, Open and Paid.

At the top of Open:

  • Players Owe: what players owe, $52, from 1 player.
  • You Owe: what the cash box owes, $70.

Below, one card per person, with their total. The cash box has no bell: what it owes is yours to pay. The cash box has its own card, Cash Box, for what you owe. Under each, their open transfers with the session's date, oldest first.

Mark a payment

The next day Sam tells Dave he paid Marcus.

  1. Under Sam, tap Sam → Marcus. The same keys as in Settle Up open, with the full amount.
  2. Confirmed By is a menu, for a transfer between players only, since you didn't see that money move: Marcus confirmed, Sam showed it or I saw it myself. It starts on Marcus, who gets the money.
  3. Tap Mark as Paid.

With the full amount the button says Mark as Paid. With less, it says Record Payment, and the rest stays open. The transfer moves to Paid. Sam now owes $30.

A partial payment

Dave sends Tyler $20 of his $70.

  1. Under Cash Box, tap Cash Box → Tyler.
  2. Tap ⌫ until the amount is gone, then type 20. Under it: $50 stays open.
  3. Tap Record Payment.

The transfer stays in the list with what's left, $50, and paid $20 of $70 under it. You Owe is down to $50.

Touch and hold a transfer for more choices: Mark as Paid for the full amount at once, Mark Partial Payment, and Open Session to go to the session it's from. A transfer between players also has Ask with the name of the one who gets the money, like Ask Marcus: it opens the share sheet with a message to Marcus asking whether Sam paid him. Nothing is sent until you send it.

When the last open transfer of a session is marked, that session finishes on its own.

Remind a player

The bell next to a player's name writes one message with everything they owe, across all sessions, and opens the share sheet, the iPhone's panel for sending things. Pick the app you message players with. Nothing is sent until you send it.

The preview shows only the first line. Sam's message starts with Hi Sam,, says how many open transfers he has from Maple Street Game, lists each one, like To the cash box: $30 with the session's date, and ends with Thanks!

If you set Your Payment Link in Settings, under Getting Paid, a message about money owed to the cash box includes it, so players can pay you right away.

In Settle Up, the bell next to a single transfer reminds a player about that one payment only.

Paid

The Paid tab lists paid transfers by month, with the month's total. Each shows the session and when it was paid. A transfer between players also shows who confirmed it, like Nov 23 session, paid Nov 24, Marcus confirmed. Dave settled up after midnight, so the payments of the night say paid Nov 24 too. Tap one to open its session.

Weekly reminders

Tap the gear at the top right of the Lobby to open Settings. Under Notifications:

  • Who Owes Reminders sends you a weekly reminder while anyone owes.
  • Game Reminders reminds you a day before a planned game and two hours before it starts. On game day it also tells you who still owes from past sessions.

These are notifications on your phone, not messages to players.

Questions

Does an unpaid player get blocked from the next game? No. What they owe shows under their name on their member screen, when you seat them and when you sell them a rebuy. It doesn't block anything.

Sam owes from two sessions. Both show under Sam, oldest first, and the bell sends one message for both.

Why isn't You Owe added to Players Owe? They're different debts: players owe the cash box or each other, and you owe players from the cash box.

I marked a payment by mistake. Open the session with Open Session or from Paid, and fix it in its settlement, see Marking Transfers Done.
