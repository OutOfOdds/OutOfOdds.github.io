Your Club

Club Stats

Club Stats shows how your club plays as a whole: how much money goes through the table, the prize pools of your tournaments, what the house keeps, and who's up and who's down. Pick a year, the last 90 or 30 days, or all time.

The example club

All the screenshots come from the Maple Street Game, Dave's home game on Maple Street.

  • The club: Dave hosts for Marcus, Jenna, Tyler, Priya and Sam. They play No-Limit Hold'em at 4/8 blinds, and now and then a $50 tournament.
  • The history: six cash games since last year, five of them this year, and two tournaments in November. Dave took rake in three of this year's cash games and the dealer got tips in one.
  • What Dave wants to know: how this year went for the club, and who's leading.

Open Club Stats

Open the club from the Lobby. Next to Members is the Stats tile, with the total buy-ins for the period: here $2,800, total buy-ins in 2026. Tap it.

Club Stats opens on this year if the club played this year, otherwise on all time. It counts sessions that are settled or waiting to be settled up. A game that's still running isn't counted yet.

Total Buy-ins

The first card is about the cash games: all the money players bought in for, rebuys included. Under the title, how many cash games the period has: 5 sessions.

  • The bars show the buy-ins month by month, from the first game of the period to the last. Touch and hold a bar to see the month, the amount and the number of sessions. With only one month, there are no bars.
  • per session: the average buy-ins of one game, here $560.
  • players per session: how many played on average, here 5.
  • rake, tips and expenses: the totals for the period, shown only when they're more than zero. Here $60 of rake and $10 of tips; nobody added an expense.

Tap an i to see what its number means. The same words are in the Glossary.

Prize Pools and House

Prize Pools shows up when the period has tournaments: the prize pools added together, $600 for 2 tournaments, the average pool per tournament, players per tournament, and entries, re-entries included. Tips and the house fee aren't part of a prize pool. See Prize Pool and Payouts.

House is what the house kept: rake and the tournaments' house fee, minus rakeback and expenses paid from the rake. Here it's $120, kept by the house in 5 sessions: $60 of rake from the cash games and $60 of house fee from the two tournaments, with no rakeback and no expenses from the rake. Dealer tips are the dealer's money and aren't counted. See Rake, Tips and Rakeback.

Results

Results lists the members by their result for the period, cash games and tournaments together, from the top: place, name, how many sessions and tournaments they played, and the result, green for up and red for down. Jenna leads 2026 with +$341.50, Sam is last with −$292.

Tap a member to open their own stats for the same period, see Player Stats and CSV.

A member you deleted from the club isn't on the list.

Change the period

The button at the top right shows the period. Tap it and choose All Time, one of the years the club played, or the last 90 days or 30 days.

All time adds last year's game: $3,200 in 6 sessions, $533 per session. A period with no games says No Sessions in This Period, with Show All Time to go back.

Good to know

  • A new club shows No Sessions Yet until its first cash game or tournament gets to the settling up.
  • A club with only tournaments shows Prize Pools, House and Results, without Total Buy-ins. Its Stats tile shows the prize pools instead of buy-ins.
  • On the iPad, Stats in the sidebar lists your clubs; pick one to see its stats next to the list.
