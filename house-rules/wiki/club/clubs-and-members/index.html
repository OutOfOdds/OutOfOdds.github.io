Your Club

Clubs and Members

A club is the group you play with: your regulars, the office game, the cousins at Thanksgiving. Its members are the players in it. Add them once, and every session you run in the club builds their history: how many games they played, how much they won or lost, and when they last showed up.

This page shows how to create a club, add and manage its members, and keep private tags and notes on them. Each member's numbers are in Player Stats and CSV.

The example club

All the screenshots come from the Maple Street Game, Dave's home game on Maple Street. Dave is the host, so in this story you are Dave.

  • The start: Dave has just installed House Rules and creates the club.
  • A month later: the club has had four cash games with six regulars: Dave, Marcus, Jenna, Tyler, Priya and Sam. Dave adds Kevin, a friend from work who's coming next time, marks Tyler as a regular and writes himself a note about him.

Create a club

The Lobby is the screen House Rules opens on. A session is one game night. When there's no club yet, the Lobby shows No Sessions Yet with two ways in: Create Club to set the club up first, or Start Session at the bottom to go straight to tonight's game. Dave taps Create Club.

If you tap Start Session instead, House Rules puts the game in a club named Home Game. To rename it later, open the club, tap the three dots at the top right, then Rename.

Create Club opens a card with a name field over the keyboard. Type the club's name, here Maple Street Game, and tap a color under it. The color marks the club's name and its sessions in lists, which helps once you have more than one club. Tap the check mark at the right of the name field to save.

The new club opens. It says 0 members, 0 sessions, and Set House Rules is at the bottom: the club's buy-in limits and rules, see House Rules. The + button at the bottom right of the club's screen starts the club's first session.

A new club has no Members tile yet: it appears with the first session. The players you seat at the table in that session become the club's members. See Starting a Cash Game.

Once you have a club, the Lobby lists it under Clubs. To make another club, tap the + next to Clubs.

The club in the Lobby

A month later the Lobby lists the Maple Street Game under Clubs: 6 members, 4 sessions, last played Nov 17. Tap the club to open it.

Touch and hold a club in the Lobby to Rename or Delete it. Deleting a club deletes all its sessions and members, and it can't be undone, so House Rules asks first.

The club's screen

The club's screen has everything about the club in one place:

  • Plan Next Game at the top, see Planned Games and Reminders.
  • The Members tile: how many there are, and the biggest winner this year. Here Top: Jenna, up $236.50 in 2026.
  • The Stats tile: $2,400 in buy-ins in 2026.
  • Set House Rules, see House Rules.
  • Recent Sessions, the club's games, newest first.

The three dots at the top right open More: Rename and Color change the club's name and color, and the menu also has Plan Game, All Games, New Season and Hide House Rules, which hides the House Rules card on the club's screen if you don't use it.

The members list

Tap the Members tile. Each row shows the member's name, how many sessions they played, when they last played, and their total result for all time: green for up, red for down.

Add a member

Players join the club when you seat them at the table, so you rarely need to add anyone by hand. To add someone before their first game, tap the + next to the member count at the top of the list, type the name and tap the check mark. Dave adds Kevin.

Kevin is now in the list with No sessions yet. Next game, Dave finds him among the club's members when he seats players.

Two members can't have the same name in one club. If the name is taken, House Rules says so under the field. Add a last initial, like Sam K.

A member's screen

Tap a member to open their screen. Tyler's shows his Result for 2026, −$92, a chart of how it went game by game, and the tiles: 4 sessions played, 50% winning sessions, −$23 per session and $0 rakeback. All of it is in Player Stats and CSV.

Tags and a note

Under the numbers is Tags and Note, with the reminder Only you see these. Tags and notes stay in House Rules on your phone: they're never shared, sent to players or exported.

Tap Add Tags. Every club starts with four tags: Backup, New, Pays Late and Regular. The number next to each says how many members have it. Tap a tag to give it to this member, tap it again to take it away. Dave taps Regular, and a check mark shows next to it. Tap the round check mark at the top right of the list to close it.

  • New Tag makes your own, with a name and a color.
  • Swipe a tag to Edit or Delete it. This changes the tag for the whole club: deleting it takes it off every member who has it, and the members stay in the club.

Then tap Add Note and write whatever helps you host. Dave writes Pays by Venmo. Brings the good chips. and taps the check mark. A note can be up to 500 characters.

Both show on Tyler's screen now: the Regular tag and the note.

In the members list, the tag shows under the member's name. The button at the top right of the list filters by tag: pick a tag to see only those members, for example only the regulars when you're inviting people to a game.

Rename or delete a member

Touch and hold a member in the list. A menu opens with Rename and Delete.

Rename fixes a typo or changes a nickname. The member's past games stay with them under the new name.

Delete asks first: Delete Member? The member will be removed from the club. Their past sessions stay as they are. Tap Delete Marcus to go ahead. There's no Cancel button: tap anywhere outside the box to keep him. Dave keeps him.

The past sessions keep the deleted member's name and amounts, so those sessions, the club's buy-in totals and the settle-ups don't change. The member just has no row in the members list and no stats screen any more.

Good to know

  • Results count only sessions that ended: settled, or waiting for settlement. A game still in progress isn't counted yet.
  • The same person in two clubs is two separate members, each with their own stats.
  • To count a player's games for a club, seat them as that club's member. A guest name typed at the table becomes a member of the club too.
