Your Club

House Rules

Every home game has its rules: how much you can buy in for, whether phones stay off the table, who pays for the pizza. House Rules is where the club keeps them. You write them once, and House Rules shows them with every session, sends them to players as text or a picture, and reminds you at the table when a buy-in is outside the limits.

The rules only remind, they never stop anything. If a player buys in above the max, House Rules says so, and you still decide.

This page shows how to set the buy-in limits, write the rules, pick the money settings, share the rules with players, and what the rules do during a game. Creating the club itself is in Clubs and Members.

The example club

All the screenshots come from the Maple Street Game, Dave's home game on Maple Street. Dave is the host, so in this story you are Dave.

  • The club: six regulars and four cash games so far, all No-Limit Hold'em at 4/8 blinds: a small blind of 4 chips and a big blind of 8, the second number. Each chip is worth $0.25, so 400 chips cost $100. The chip value is set when you start a session, see Chip Value.
  • What happens: Dave writes down the rules everyone has been following anyway: a buy-in from 50 to 150 big blinds, phones off the table, pay the host before you leave, straddles allowed, last orbit at 1 a.m., and cash on the table before you get chips. Then he sends the rules to the group, and at the next game House Rules reminds him of them.

Open House Rules

In the Lobby, the screen House Rules opens on, tap the club under Clubs. On the club's screen, tap Set House Rules under the Members and Stats tiles.

The House Rules screen has four parts:

  • All Games: rules for every game the club plays. Shown with every session.
  • Cash Games: the buy-in limits, Min Buy-in and Max Buy-in, and rules only for cash games.
  • Money: four settings for paying, food, short chips and the host, used in cash games.
  • Tournaments: rules only for tournaments. A tournament's buy-in, starting stack and re-entries come from its own settings, not from here. See Setting Up a Tournament.

There's no Save button. Everything you change here is saved right away.

The buy-in limits

The limits are in big blinds (BB), not in chips or money. A buy-in of 50 BB is 50 times the big blind, so the same rule works whatever blinds you play: at 4/8 it's 400 chips, at 1/2 it's 100. House Rules turns it into chips with each session's blinds.

Tap the empty box at the right of the Min Buy-in row. A keypad opens. Type 50. The card above the keys says In big blinds, and the line under it shows what that means at the club's last blinds: At 4/8, 400 chips. The keys on the right are shortcuts: 50, 100 and 200 big blinds. Tap the check mark to save.

Then tap the box at the right of Max Buy-in and type 150: At 4/8, 1,200 chips. The max has its own shortcuts, bigger ones: 200, 300 and 500 big blinds.

Both rows now show 50 BB and 150 BB, and the line under the Cash Games card turns them into chips and money: At 4/8 that's 400 to 1,200 chips, $100 to $300. The money comes from the chip value of the club's last game, here $0.25 a chip.

A few things to know:

  • You can set only one of them: only a max, or only a min.
  • The min can't be bigger than the max. If it is, the line under the number turns red and the check mark waits until you fix it. Min and max can be the same.
  • A limit can be from 1 to 10,000 big blinds.
  • To clear a limit, open it, delete the number and tap the check mark.

Write the rules

Every section has Add Rule. Tap it in All Games. A text field opens over the keyboard, with ready-made rules above it and a choice of All Games, Cash or Tournaments under it. Tap a ready-made rule to add it, or type your own. Dave taps Phones off the table, then Pay the host before you leave.

The ready-made rules change with the section. Under Cash they're poker rules like Run it twice, No rabbit hunting and Stay an hour after a big win. Dave taps Straddle allowed, then types his own: Last orbit at 1 a.m., with Cash picked under the field, and taps the check mark.

The rules are on the screen now: two under All Games, two under Cash Games.

To change the rules later:

  • Tap a rule to change its text. Pick another section under the field to move the rule there. Cash under the field means the Cash Games section.
  • Edit at the right of a section's title, shown once the section has rules, lets you delete rules or drag them into a new order. You can also swipe a rule to the left to delete it.
  • A section holds up to 20 rules. A ready-made rule you've already added isn't offered again.

Money

The Money settings are for cash games. Each is a menu: tap it and pick an option.

  • Paying: how players pay for chips. Cash Up Front, Transfer Up Front or Settle at End. It picks the payment on the buy-in and rebuy keys: with Cash Up Front, every buy-in starts as Cash. See Buy-Ins, Rebuys and Cash-Outs.
  • Food: who shares a new expense. Split Equally checks everyone, Host Pays only the host, Winners Pay the players who are up. You can still change it on each expense. See Expenses and Splitting Them.
  • Short Chips: who pays the difference when the chips counted at the end are fewer than the chips handed out, for example when a chip fell under the table: Winners, Everyone or the Host. It's the first choice when you close the session, and you can pick another there. See Close Session, Step by Step.
  • Host: the club member who hosts, picked from the club's members. Food and Short Chips use it when they're set to the host.

Dave picks Cash Up Front, Split Equally and Dave as the host. He leaves Short Chips at Not Set.

Send the rules to players

Tap the share button at the top right, a square with an arrow pointing up. Pick Share as Text or Share as Image.

  • Share as Text sends the rules as a message: the All Games rules first, then Cash games: with the buy-in, here Buy-in: 50 to 150 BB (400 to 1,200 chips at 4/8, $100 to $300), the money settings and the cash rules, then the tournament rules.
  • Share as Image sends the same as a picture in a dark card, ready for a group chat.

Both open the iPhone's share sheet. Pick the app or the person to send it to.

Settings you didn't set aren't in the message. The share button is dimmed only while the screen is completely empty: no rules, no buy-in limits and no Money settings.

The House Rules card

Back on the club's screen, Set House Rules has turned into a House Rules card. It shows the paying setting, Cash up front, the buy-in, Buy-in 50–150 BB, the first two rules and +2 more for the rest. Tap it to open the rules.

If you don't use House Rules, tap the three dots at the top right of the club's screen and Hide House Rules. The card goes away. To bring it back, pick House Rules in the same menu.

At the table

The next week Dave starts a cash game at 4/8 again. See Starting a Cash Game. When he adds Jenna, her buy-in is already 400, the min, and it costs $100. Payment is set to Cash, from the Paying setting. The keys on the right have changed too: 400 min, 1,200 max, and 800, which is 100 BB, halfway.

Marcus wants 1,600. Dave types it, and House Rules says Above the max buy-in of 1,200 under the number. The check mark still works: the rules only remind. Dave tells Marcus about the limit, and Marcus buys in for 1,200.

How each move is checked:

  • A buy-in and Back in Game are checked against both limits: Below the min buy-in or Above the max buy-in.
  • A rebuy is checked only against the max, because a rebuy can be small.
  • A cash-out and the rake aren't checked.

During a game, the session's More menu has House Rules too, with the Cash Games and All Games rules. Changes there apply to the whole club, not just this session. A tournament's More menu shows the Tournaments and All Games rules, with the tournament's own buy-in and re-entries.

Good to know

  • The rules belong to the club. Every game in the club uses them, and a session outside a club has none.
  • The limits follow each session's blinds. At 2/4 the same 50 to 150 BB is 200 to 600 chips.
  • If the club hasn't played a game with blinds yet, the screen explains the limits in big blinds instead of showing chips.
  • When the club's host is deleted from the club, Host goes back to Not Set.
