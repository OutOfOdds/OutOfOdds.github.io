Your Club

Planned Games and Reminders

The hardest part of a home game is getting people to the table. A week before, the group chat says five are in, two are maybe and three haven't answered. A planned game keeps all of it in one place: when and where you play, the seats, and who said they're coming. On the night, you start the session right from it.

House Rules doesn't send anything to players by itself and players don't answer in the app. You send the invite to your group chat, they reply there as usual, and you mark each reply. House Rules reminds you, the host, before the game.

This page shows how to plan a game, invite players, keep track of replies and the waitlist, and start the session on game day. Clubs and members are in Clubs and Members.

The example club

All the screenshots come from the Maple Street Game, Dave's home game on Maple Street. Dave is the host, so in this story you are Dave.

  • The club: six regulars, Dave, Marcus, Jenna, Tyler, Priya and Sam, who play No-Limit Hold'em (NLH) at 4/8 blinds every Tuesday: a small blind of 4 chips and a big blind of 8. A chip is worth $0.25.
  • Today: Tuesday, November 24, the week after the club's last game.
  • What happens: Dave plans next Tuesday's game at his place, with five seats at the table. Over the week he marks the replies. Marcus asks to bring a friend, Nick, but the table is full, so Nick waits for a seat. Then Priya can't make it, and Nick gets her seat.

Plan a game

In the Lobby, the screen House Rules opens on, tap the club under Clubs. If you have no club yet, see Clubs and Members. When the club has no game planned, Plan Next Game is at the top of the club's screen. Tap it. You can also tap the three dots at the top right of the club's screen, then Plan Game. It works even when a game is already planned, and adds another one.

New Game opens with most of it filled in:

  • The name: Tuesday Game, from the day of the week, shown in gray. Type over it to call the game something else.
  • Starts: the next 7:00 PM that's at least an hour away. Tap the date or the time to change them.
  • Where: empty, type the place.
  • Cash Game or Tournament, at the top of the Game section. For a cash game there are two more rows: Game, the kind of poker, NLH (No-Limit Hold'em) or PLO (Pot-Limit Omaha), and Stakes: NLH 4/8, 1 chip = $0.25, the blinds and chip value of the club's last session. You can change them when you start the session. For a tournament you set the buy-in and the blinds when you start it.
  • Seat Limit: off. Turn it on to set how many players fit at the table.
  • Note: anything players should know.
  • Invite All Members: on, so the club's members are on the list from the start.

Dave taps the date and picks Tuesday, December 1, a week from today. The time stays at 7:00 PM.

He types Dave's place, 14 Maple St. under Where, turns on Seat Limit and sets Seats to 5 with the minus button: his table fits five. When the seats are taken, anyone else who says they're going joins the waitlist. Under Note he writes Pizza at 7. Cash for the buy-in, please. Invite All Members stays on: 6 members.

Tap the check mark at the top right, Add Game, to save.

The game's screen

The game opens. At the top: its name, the club and the time, Tue, Dec 1, 7:00 PM. Under it, the seats: 0 of 5 in big numbers, with seats taken, 5 left under them. Then Details with Where, Game and Note, and Players: 6 invited, all six under No Reply for now, the host too. At the bottom are Invite and Start Session.

The first time you plan a game, your phone asks whether House Rules may send notifications. Tap Allow to get the reminders described at the end of this page.

Mark the replies

Players answer in your chat, and you mark each answer. Tap a player's row. A menu opens with Going, Maybe, Out and No Reply, and Remove to take the player off the list.

By Thursday, five players are in: Dave, Marcus, Jenna, Tyler and Priya. Sam says maybe. The list sorts itself into groups, Going first, then Maybe, No Reply and Out, each with a count.

Add a player

Add Player under the list adds someone who isn't invited yet:

  • Club Members picks from the club's members who aren't on the list. Here it's dimmed, because everyone is invited.
  • Guest adds someone by name. A guest is on this game's list only and doesn't become a club member.

Marcus wants to bring his friend Nick. Dave taps Guest, types Nick and taps the check mark.

The waitlist

Nick says he's going, and Dave marks him Going. But all five seats are taken, so Nick goes to the Waitlist. The waitlist shows only when the game has a seat limit. Players on it are in the order you marked them Going.

On Monday Priya texts that she can't come. Dave marks her Out. Her seat goes to the first player on the waitlist, and House Rules says so: Nick moved off the waitlist. Tell Nick yourself, House Rules doesn't message players.

If you lower the seat limit later, with Edit in the game's More menu, the players you marked Going last move to the waitlist.

Invite players

Tap Invite at the bottom. There are two ways to send the game:

  • Invite: a message for your group chat.
  • Calendar Event: a calendar file. Players who open it can add the game to their own calendar.

Both open the iPhone's share sheet, the panel of apps and people to send to. Pick the app your group uses, like Messages or WhatsApp, and the chat. The message has the game's details and the latest count of players and seats. Dave's reads:

Tuesday Game, Tue, Dec 1, 7:00 PM
Dave's place, 14 Maple St.
NLH 4/8, 1 chip = $0.25
Pizza at 7. Cash for the buy-in, please.
5 going, no seats left. Reply here: in, maybe or out.

The last line always has the latest count, so you can send the invite again later to show who's coming and how many seats are left.

Edit, add to your calendar or cancel

The three dots at the top right of the game open More:

  • Edit opens the game's details again: the name, time, place, game, seats and note.
  • Add to Calendar puts the game in your own calendar. A New Event card opens with the name, the time from 7:00 PM to 11:00 PM, four hours, and the place. Tap the check mark to add it.
  • Cancel Game asks first. The game stays in the list as cancelled. Let the players know yourself.

Where the game shows up

Back on the club's screen, Plan Next Game has turned into the Next Game card: Tue, Dec 1, 7:00 PM, the place and the game, a bar of the seats taken, and 5 going, 1 maybe, 0 no reply. 5 seats is at the top right. Tap the card to open the game.

In the Lobby, Upcoming lists the next games of all your clubs, up to three: Tue, Dec 1, 7:00 PM, Maple Street Game, 5 of 5 going. All Games under it lists every planned game, including past and cancelled ones. On game day, the line under Lobby says Game tonight.

Start the session

On Tuesday night, open the game and tap Start Session. New Session opens with the game's club, blinds and chip value, and the line From Tuesday Game, Tue, Dec 1. Change anything that's different tonight, then tap the check mark.

The session doesn't seat anyone by itself: add the players as they arrive, see Starting a Cash Game. The game's screen then shows Session started at and the time. Tap it to open the session.

On game day, Start Session at the bottom of the Lobby opens the same New Session for tonight's game. With two games that day, it takes the earlier one. A game that was never started shows Didn't start once its time has passed, and leaves Next Game and Upcoming. It stays in All Games.

Reminders

House Rules reminds you, the host, twice:

  • A day before: for example Tuesday Game tomorrow, with the replies so far: 5 going, 1 maybe, 0 no reply. No seats left.
  • Two hours before: Game in 2 hours, Tuesday Game, 5 going. Tap it to open the game.

They're on from the start. To turn them off, open Settings with the gear at the top right of the Lobby and turn off Game Reminders, under Games. Players don't get reminders from House Rules, only you do.

If you said Don't Allow to notifications, there are no reminders, and everything else works. To allow them later, open the iPhone's Settings app, then Apps, House Rules, Notifications.

Good to know

  • The game is planned on this phone only. Players see what you send them: the message and the calendar file.
  • Deleting a session started from a game puts the game back to planned, so you can start it again.
  • On an iPad, planned games aren't shown in the club yet. Plan them on the iPhone.
  • Planned games aren't in a .pokersession file when you move sessions to another phone.
