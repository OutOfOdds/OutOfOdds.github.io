Your Club

Player Stats and CSV

Every session you close in a club adds to its members' numbers. Each member has a screen with their result, how often they win, their average per session and every game they played, for this year, the last month or all time. You can send a player their stats as a picture, or export them as a CSV file for a spreadsheet.

This page shows how to read a member's stats, change the period, switch between cash games and tournaments, and share or export them. Adding and managing members is in Clubs and Members.

The example club

All the screenshots come from the Maple Street Game, Dave's home game on Maple Street. Dave is the host, so in this story you are Dave.

  • The club: six regulars, Dave, Marcus, Jenna, Tyler, Priya and Sam. They play No-Limit Hold'em cash games at 4/8 blinds with a chip worth $0.25, and now and then a $50 tournament.
  • The games: six cash games, one in October 2025, one in September 2026 and four Tuesdays in a row this fall, and two tournaments, on October 31 and November 14, 2026.
  • What happens: Jenna asks how she's doing this year. Dave opens her stats, looks at a few periods, sends her a picture and exports a CSV for his own spreadsheet.

Open a member's stats

Open the club from the Lobby, the screen House Rules opens on, and tap the Members tile. It shows how many members there are and the biggest winner this year: Top: Jenna, +$371.50.

The club's 8 sessions count cash games and tournaments together. The members list shows each member's cash games (sessions) and tournaments separately, the date they last played, and their result for all time, cash games and tournaments together: green for up, red for down. Jenna is up $426.50 over 6 sessions, 2 tournaments. The button at the top right of the list shows only members with one tag. Tap Jenna's row.

Read the stats

Jenna's screen opens on 2026, this year, because she played this year. A member who hasn't played this year opens on all time. Under her name: Maple Street Game, 6 sessions, 2 tournaments, all her games, whatever the period. She played both kinds of games, so there's a switch with Cash Games and Tournaments. Cash Games is first.

  • Result for 2026: +$181.50, what she won minus what she paid for chips in this year's cash games. The result already includes rakeback, money the club gives back to a player out of the rake, and her share of expenses like food. See Rake, Tips and Rakeback and Expenses and Splitting Them.
  • The chart: her result added up game by game. Her September game was a loss of $55, so the line starts below zero, then the four Tuesdays take it to +$181.50. Touch and hold the chart to see the total after each game.
  • sessions played: 5 this year.
  • winning sessions: 60%, three of the five ended above zero.
  • per session: +$36, the result divided by the sessions and rounded to whole dollars.
  • rakeback: $0, the club doesn't give rakeback here.

Tap the small i next to a title or a tile for a one-line explanation.

The list of sessions

Scroll down. Under the tags and note, which are in Clubs and Members, are Credit and Payment, see Who Owes and Reminders, and Sessions: every game in the period, newest first, with her buy-ins in chips and her result. On Tuesday, November 17 she bought in for 400 chips, $100 at $0.25 a chip, cashed out $75 and lost $25.00. On November 10 she won $177.00. Tap a session to open it.

Change the period

The button at the top right, 2026, picks the period. It lists All Time, each year the member played, newest first, here 2026 and 2025, then 90 days and 30 days.

With 30 days, the title says Result for Last 30 Days: +$236.50 from the 4 Tuesday games, 75% of them winning, +$59 per session. The September loss is older than 30 days, so it's not counted.

With All Time, the title says All-Time Result: +$236.50 from 6 sessions. Last year's win of $55 and September's loss of $55 cancel out. 66.7% of her sessions were winning, and +$39 per session.

The days count back from today, today included. A period with no games says No Sessions in This Period, with Show All Time to go back.

Tournaments

Tap Tournaments on the switch. Tournaments have their own numbers, because a tournament is paid for differently: everyone pays the same buy-in, and the prizes come from the pool. See Prize Pool and Payouts.

Jenna's All-Time Tournament Result is +$190:

  • tournaments played: 2.
  • in the money: 100%, 2 of 2. She won a prize both times.
  • wins: 1, finished first.
  • ROI: +173%, the result divided by everything she paid in. She paid $55 each time, the $50 buy-in that goes to the prize pool and a $5 fee for the house, set up with the tournament. Paid includes both, so $110 in all, and $190 is 173% of $110.

Below, the list of Tournaments shows each one with her place and what she paid: on Saturday, November 14 she was 2nd of 6, paid $55, and is up $50.

The total on the Members tile and in the members list adds cash games and tournaments together: Jenna's +$426.50 for all time is $236.50 from cash games and $190 from tournaments.

Send a player their stats

The share button at the top right has two choices: Share Stats and Export CSV.

Share Stats makes a card to send to the player. At the top, pick Image, a picture, or Text, the same numbers as plain text to paste into a message. Under Include, pick the Period and what goes on the card: Cash Games, Tournaments and Last Sessions, her five most recent games. The Preview below shows the card: here Maple Street Game, 2026, Jenna, and a Total Result of +$371.50, cash games and tournaments together for this year: $181.50 from cash games and $190 from tournaments. The Members tile shows the same this-year total. Tap the check mark to send it from the share sheet.

Tags and notes never go on the card. The club remembers which parts you included, so the next card starts the same way.

Export a CSV

Export CSV makes a spreadsheet file of the tab you're on and the period you picked, and opens the share sheet. Save it to Files, mail it to yourself, or open it in Numbers, Excel or Google Sheets. Here it's Stats, Jenna, her cash games in 2026.

The file is named after the member and the time, for example player_stats_jenna_20261124_1530.csv. Each line has a section, then the item and its value:

  • meta: the member, the club, when the file was made, and the period.
  • summary: Sessions Played, Winning Sessions, Total Result, Per Session, and Result to Buy-ins, the result divided by what she paid for chips.
  • turnover: Buy-ins, Cash-outs and Rakeback for the period.
  • session: one line per game with its date, buy-ins, cash-out and result.

Money is written as plain numbers like 181.50, without the currency sign, so a spreadsheet can add them up. On the Tournaments tab, Export CSV makes a separate file, tournament_stats_jenna_...csv, with tournaments played, in the money, wins, ROI, paid in, prizes, and one line per tournament.

What counts

  • Only the club's sessions that are settled or waiting for settlement count. A game still in progress isn't in the stats yet.
  • The result is in money, at each session's chip value, so games with different chip values add up correctly.
  • Rakeback and expense shares are already in the result.
  • A member's stats belong to one club. The same person in two clubs has separate stats in each.

Good to know

  • On the iPhone you export one member at a time. On the iPad, Export CSV at the top of the club's screen exports all members at once.
  • The Stats tile on the club's screen is about the whole club, not one member: the total buy-ins for the period.
  • A member who hasn't played yet shows No Sessions Yet. Their tags and note still work.
