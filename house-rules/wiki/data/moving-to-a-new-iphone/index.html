Your Data

Moving to a New iPhone

Got a new iPhone? Your clubs, players and every past session can come with you. There are two ways: iCloud brings them over by itself, and a backup file works with or without iCloud.

The example club

The screenshots come from Dave's phones. Dave hosts the Maple Street Game on Tuesdays for Marcus, Jenna, Tyler, Priya and Sam, and has a few weeks of sessions in House Rules on his old iPhone. He moves them to a new one.

With iCloud: just wait

If Sync with iCloud is on on your old iPhone, which it is unless you turned it off, there's nothing to move:

  1. On the new iPhone, sign in with the same Apple Account as on the old one.
  2. Install House Rules from the App Store and open it.
  3. Your clubs and sessions come down from iCloud by themselves. A club with a long history can take a while the first time, up to an hour or more. Keep House Rules open and the iPhone on Wi-Fi until everything is there.

To check, open Settings on the new iPhone and look at the line under Sync with iCloud, see Sync with iCloud. A simulator can't sign in to iCloud, so there are no screenshots of this way here.

Before you wipe the old iPhone, open House Rules on it once more with internet on, so its last changes go up to iCloud. Then check on the new iPhone that your last session is there.

With a backup file

Use a backup if iCloud sync is off, if you play under another Apple Account on the new iPhone, or if you'd simply like to have a copy.

On the old iPhone

Open Settings, tap Back Up All Data and save the file somewhere the new iPhone can reach: Save to Files in iCloud Drive, AirDrop to the new iPhone, or your Mac. See Back Up and Restore.

On the new iPhone

Open House Rules. With nothing in it yet, the Lobby is empty.

Restore the file

  1. Open Settings and tap Restore from Backup, under Data.
  2. Pick the backup file. If you got it with AirDrop, it's in Files, in Downloads.
  3. The Restore Backup sheet shows what's in the file: the date, the device it came from, and how many clubs, members, sessions and tournaments it has. On an empty iPhone the choice under How to Restore doesn't matter: either way you get everything from the file.
  4. Tap the check mark.

Set these again

A few settings live on the iPhone itself, so neither iCloud nor a backup carries them. After the move, open Settings and check:

  • Your Payment Link, under Getting Paid.
  • The switches under Notifications: Level Change, Game Reminders, Who Owes Reminders and Lock Screen. The first time House Rules wants to send you a notification on the new iPhone, iOS asks you to allow it.

Good to know

  • With both iCloud sync and a backup, restore with Add What's Missing: what's already come down from iCloud stays, and nothing is added twice.
  • Keep the backup file after the move. It's your copy of the club as it was that day.
