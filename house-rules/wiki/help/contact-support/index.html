Help

Contact Support

Something in House Rules doesn't work the way you expect, a number looks wrong, or you have an idea? Write to us by email.

Look here first

Many questions already have an answer, and it's faster than waiting for a reply:

  • The FAQ: short answers to common questions, see FAQ.
  • Something looks off: chips that don't add up, a cash count that doesn't match, a transfer that looks wrong. See The Chips Don't Add Up, The Cash Box Doesn't Match and A Transfer Looks Wrong.
  • Help in the app: tap Settings, the gear at the top right of the Lobby, then Help at the top. It has the same topics as this wiki, a step-by-step first game, and Search articles and terms at the bottom.

Write to us

Tap Support at the bottom of any page of this site. It opens a new email to us in your mail app.

What to put in your message

The more we know, the faster we can help. Tell us:

  1. What you did, step by step, and what you expected to happen.
  2. What happened instead. Copy any message House Rules showed you word for word.
  3. Your iPhone or iPad model and its iOS version. You'll find both in the iPhone's Settings, General, About.
  4. Your House Rules version. In House Rules, open Settings and scroll to the bottom. The last line is the version with its build number in brackets, for example House Rules 1.0 (26). Copy it as it is.
  5. A screenshot of the screen, if you can.

When a number looks wrong

For a question about a session's chips, cash box or transfers, the session itself helps most. Open it, tap More, the button with three dots at the top right, then Share Session, and attach the file to your email. See Export and Import.

The file has the session as it is: your players' names, their chips and money, and the cash box. Send it only if that's fine with your players. Otherwise, a screenshot of the screen with the number is enough.

Good to know

  • House Rules has no account and doesn't send anything to us by itself. We only see what you put in your email.
  • Ideas and wishes are welcome by the same email.
