Help

Glossary

House Rules uses a few words of its own, like Cash Box, Still Owed and Left on Table, next to the usual poker ones. This page lists all 36 terms from the Glossary in the app, with a short explanation of each and the article that tells more.

In the app

Open Settings, the gear at the top right of the Lobby, tap Help and scroll down to Glossary. The terms are in alphabetical order. Tap a term to open the article about it.

The "i" next to a number

Many screens have a small i next to a heading or a number, for example rake in Club Stats. Tap it to see what the term means, right where you are. When Help has an article about the term, Learn More opens it on top of the screen; Done closes the article and leaves the note open.

All terms

  • Add-On. Extra chips a player still in can buy once per tournament. The add-on price goes to the prize pool, with no house fee. See Entries, Re-Entries and Add-Ons.
  • Already Done. Transfers paid before the plan was worked out again. The current plan already counts them, and you can't unmark them. To undo one to or from the cash box, delete its entry in Cash Box. See A Transfer Looks Wrong.
  • Average Stack. All chips in play divided by the players still in. Re-entries and add-ons raise it. See Running the Timer.
  • Best Results Count. Only each player's highest-scoring tournaments add up in the season. Ties go to more wins, then the better place in the latest tournament. See Counting Only the Best Results.
  • Bounty. Money every entry pays on top of the buy-in. Whoever knocks the player out collects it, or half of it in a progressive tournament. See Fixed Bounties and PKO.
  • Cash Box. The real money for this session. Collect when a player pays you, Pay Out when you hand money back. See The Cash Box.
  • Chip Value. How much one chip is worth in money. Results and the cash box use it. See Chip Value.
  • Close Session. Steps to finish the session: cash out everyone, record rake and tips, split expenses, collect what's owed, then settle up. See Close Session, Step by Step.
  • Dealer Button. The button doesn't move on its own. Touch and hold a seat and choose Button Here. See Tables and Seats.
  • Dealer Tips. Chips left for the dealer. They don't count toward anyone's result. In a tournament, tips are a share of the buy-ins. See Rake, Tips and Rakeback.
  • Expenses. What the session spent on food, rent and the like. Split it between players or pay it from the rake. See Expenses and Splitting Them.
  • From You. The part of the guarantee the house fee doesn't cover. You add it to the cash box. See Prize Pool and Payouts.
  • Guarantee. The least the prize pool will be. If entries fall short, the house fee covers the gap first and you add the rest. See Prize Pool and Payouts.
  • House Fee. What each entry pays the house on top of the buy-in. It goes to the cash box, not the prize pool. See Setting Up a Tournament.
  • House Rules. Your club's own rules, like the buy-in range. They show up at the table and when you share them. See House Rules.
  • Kept by House. The rake that stays with the house after rakeback and expenses paid from it. See Rake, Tips and Rakeback.
  • Leave to Play For. Money kept out of the deal so the players can play on for it. It goes to 1st place on top of their deal amount. See Making a Deal.
  • Left on Table. Chips still in play after a player cashes out. Only the last player at the table can cash out more: what's missing becomes Short Chips in Close Session. See Buy-Ins, Rebuys and Cash-Outs.
  • Left to Split. The part of the expense nobody has taken yet. Split the whole amount or nobody: the checkmark waits until this is zero. See Expenses and Splitting Them.
  • Needs Settling. Everyone has cashed out, but the session isn't finished: steps or transfers are still open. The amount shown is the session's buy-ins, not a debt. See Close Session, Step by Step.
  • New Members. Players in the file who aren't in the club. Selected players join it; for the rest, the session won't count in club stats. See Export and Import.
  • Paid Out. Everything that left the cash box: payouts to players, expenses and dealer tips. See The Cash Box.
  • Payouts. How the prize pool is split between the paid places. Until the money, the card shows how many more players must go out. See Prize Pool and Payouts.
  • Prize Pool. The money the paid places share: buy-ins and add-ons, minus dealer tips. A guarantee keeps it from going lower. See Prize Pool and Payouts.
  • Rake. Chips taken from the table for the house. You record it from the chips left over after everyone cashes out. See Rake, Tips and Rakeback.
  • Rakeback. Part of the rake you give back to players. It counts toward their result. See Rake, Tips and Rakeback.
  • Re-Entry. A fresh stack for a player who busts while re-entry is still open. It costs the same as the first entry. See Entries, Re-Entries and Add-Ons.
  • Results. Chips cashed out minus chips bought, at your chip value. Unpaid buy-ins don't change a result: they go into the transfers. See How Settle Up Works.
  • Round To. Every place but 1st rounds down to this step. What's left over goes to 1st. See Prize Pool and Payouts.
  • Season Points. Points a player earns in each settled club tournament within the season's dates. They add up in the season's Standings. See How Season Points Work.
  • Seat Limit. When the seats fill up, anyone else who says Going joins the waitlist in order. See Planned Games and Reminders.
  • Settle Up. Who pays whom after the session, in a short list of transfers. Mark each one as done when the money moves. See How Settle Up Works.
  • Short Chips. Chips missing after everyone cashes out. Winners pay in proportion to what they won, Everyone splits equally, or Host covers it all. See The Chips Don’t Add Up.
  • Should Be in Cash. Cash payments minus cash payouts. Money sent by transfer went to your bank, so it isn't counted. See The Cash Box Doesn’t Match.
  • Still Owed. Buy-ins and expense shares players haven't paid you yet. Collecting is optional: what's left unpaid goes into the transfers. See When a Player Pays Later.
  • Who Owes. Transfers from past sessions that nobody has marked as done yet. See Who Owes and Reminders.
