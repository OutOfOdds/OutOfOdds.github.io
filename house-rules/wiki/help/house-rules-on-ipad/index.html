House Rules on iPad

House Rules runs on the iPad with a layout of its own: a sidebar on the left with everything the Lobby has on the iPhone, and the game itself spread over the rest of the screen. With a keyboard, the main actions have shortcuts. Your clubs and sessions are the same as on your iPhone while Sync with iCloud is on, see Sync with iCloud.

The example club

The screenshots come from Dave's iPad. Dave hosts the Maple Street Game on Tuesdays. Tonight's game is running: Dave, Marcus, Jenna, Tyler and Priya are at the table, 2,400 chips are in play and $600 is in the cash box.

Landscape

House Rules on the iPad works in landscape: turn the iPad on its side, the layout needs the width. In a window next to other apps, it keeps a size of at least 1000 by 700 points, so the columns always fit.

The sidebar

The sidebar on the left does what the Lobby does on the iPhone:

  • Tonight: the running session, with the club and how many players are at the table, for example Live, 5 at the table. With no game running, Start Session.
  • Needs Settling: sessions where everyone has cashed out but the settling up isn't finished. The group is there only when there are some.
  • Clubs: your clubs, and New Club to add one.
  • Library: All Sessions, Stats, Blind Timer and Settings.

The + at the top of the sidebar starts a new session. With no game running and nothing chosen, the right side shows No Session Running and Start Session.

A running game

Tap the running session under Tonight. The game takes the rest of the screen:

  • At the top, the club and the date, and Table, Cash Box and Settle Up to switch between the table, the money and the end of the night.
  • In the middle, a strip with the chips on the table, bought in and cashed out, the money in the cash box and what's still owed, and under it a tile for each player at the table with their chips.
  • On the right, Tonight: who's at the table and the latest buy-ins and rebuys, with Add Player and Close Session at the bottom.

Tap a player's tile to work with them. The right side turns into that player's panel: Rebuy, Cash Out and History, with the keys to type the chips. Tap the × to go back to the game.

Cash Box shows the money of the session: what's in the cash box, what's been collected, what's still owed, the rake and the expenses, and every payment in Operations. On the right, Before You Settle Up, with Collect and Pay Out. See The Cash Box.

Tap the club's name at the top for the session's menu: Edit Session, Rake and Tips, House Rules, Session Log, Share Session, Hand Over Session and Delete Session.

Settle Up, or Close Session, walks you through the end of the night over the whole right side, the same steps as on the iPhone, see Close Session, Step by Step.

Keyboard shortcuts

With a keyboard attached, these shortcuts work. The menu bar at the top of the screen lists them too, in the File, View and Session menus.

  • ⌘N: New Session
  • ⌘T: Blind Timer
  • ⇧⌘T: Open Blind Timer in New Window
  • ⌘1: Table
  • ⌘2: Cash Box
  • ⌘3: Settle Up
  • ⌘R: Rebuy for the chosen player, or Back in Game for a player who has cashed out
  • ⌘O: Cash Out for the chosen player

The Session menu also has Rake and Tips and Close Session, without shortcuts. A menu item is greyed out when it doesn't fit the screen you're on, for example Rebuy with no player chosen.

More on the iPad

  • The blind timer in its own window: keep the big clock in a window of its own while you run the game. See Timer Window on iPad.
  • Drop a session file on the window: drag a .pokersession file from Files or Mail onto House Rules to import it. See Dropping a File on iPad.

Good to know

  • The iPad and the iPhone are the same app with the same data. You can start a game on the iPad and check it on your phone, as long as both sync with iCloud.
