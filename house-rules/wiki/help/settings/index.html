Help

Settings

Settings is one screen with everything that isn't about a club or a session: help, your data, notifications, your payment link and importing a session from another host. This page goes through it from top to bottom and says where each part is explained in full.

Open Settings

Tap the gear at the top right of the Lobby. On the iPad, Settings is at the bottom of the sidebar.

Help

The first row opens Help in the app: the same topics as this wiki, a step-by-step first cash game and first tournament, How a Cash Game Flows in six steps, common questions, the Glossary, and Contact Support at the bottom. Search articles and questions finds an answer by a word. See Glossary and Contact Support.

Data

  • Sync with iCloud keeps your clubs, sessions, cash boxes and the timer the same on all your iPhones and iPads. It's on from the start. The line under it says how syncing is going. See Sync with iCloud.
  • Back Up All Data saves everything to one file you keep yourself. Once you have one, the date of your last backup shows under it.
  • Restore from Backup brings a backup file back, on this iPhone or a new one. See Back Up and Restore.

Notifications

Four switches, each with a line saying what it does:

  • Level Change: a notification when the blind timer moves to the next level on its own. See Notifications and Lock Screen.
  • Game Reminders: a day and two hours before a planned game. On game day it also tells you who still owes from past sessions. See Planned Games and Reminders.
  • Who Owes Reminders: once a week, while anyone in your clubs owes. Off from the start. See Who Owes and Reminders.
  • Lock Screen: a running cash game and the blind timer as a Live Activity, a live card on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. See Lock Screen and Quick Rebuy.

If notifications for House Rules are off in the iPhone's Settings app, a row Turn On Notifications shows at the top of this group, in the warning color. Tap it to go straight to the iPhone's notification settings for House Rules.

Getting Paid

Your Payment Link is a link players can pay you by, for example your page in a payment app. Tap the row, paste the link and tap the check mark. House Rules adds it to the reminders you send when someone owes the cash box, so players can pay with one tap. For a PayPal.me link, the amount is added too. While there's no link, the row says None.

The link stays on this iPhone: it isn't synced through iCloud, isn't in a backup and doesn't go into a session file.

Sessions from Other Hosts

Import Session brings in a .pokersession file that another host sent you. To send one of yours, open the session or tournament and choose Share Session in the More menu. See Export and Import.

About

  • Share House Rules sends a link to House Rules to a friend.
  • More Apps opens our other apps in the App Store.

The last line of Settings is the version of House Rules with its build number in brackets, for example House Rules 1.0 (26). Put it in your email when you write to support.

Good to know

  • Settings on the iPhone itself, like the payment link and the notification switches, aren't synced through iCloud and aren't in a backup. On a new iPhone, set them again, see Moving to a New iPhone.
  • House Rules has no account and no sign-in. Your data stays on your devices and in your own iCloud.
