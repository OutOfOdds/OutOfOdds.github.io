Money

Expenses and Splitting Them

An expense is money spent on the night itself: pizza, drinks, the room, a new deck. Someone pays for it, and usually everyone at the table shares the cost. House Rules keeps track of both: who paid and who shares.

You record an expense in the cash box, the session's money (see The Cash Box). Each player's share comes off their result, what they won or lost tonight. The one who paid gets the full amount back. Nobody hands cash around during the game: it all comes out at settle-up, when House Rules works out who pays whom at the end of the night.

The example

It's two and a half hours into a night at the Maple Street Game.

  • The table: Dave, the host, with Jenna, Marcus, Priya and Tyler. The cash box holds $600.
  • What happens: Dave orders pizza for $50 and pays for it with his own card.
  • Who shares: all five, equally, $10 each. So Dave gets $40 back: $50 he paid minus his own $10.

Add an expense

  1. In the session, tap Cash Box at the bottom left, and scroll down to Expenses.
  2. Tap + next to Expenses.
  3. Type the name, Pizza, and tap the arrow on the right.
  4. Type the amount, 50, and tap the checkmark at the top right of the keys.

The toast says Expense recorded, Pizza, $50, with Undo.

The cash box still holds $600: Dave paid with his own money, not from the cash box. Under Before You Settle Up, a line Pizza, not split, $50 waits for you to say who paid and who shares.

Split it

Tap the expense, either the line under Before You Settle Up or the row under Expenses. Split Expense opens, with three steps from top to bottom.

Step 1, Paid From. Choose where the money came from:

  • Player: one of the players paid. Pick them in the list below.
  • Cash Box: you paid it from the session's money. The cash box must hold enough to cover all of it.
  • Rake: paid from the rake, the house's share of the chips, see Rake, Tips and Rakeback. Players don't share an expense paid from the rake.
  • Hosts: the hosts paid it themselves. The hosts are whoever runs the game, like Dave, or the house in a club.

You see Rake only once the session has rake. This one doesn't yet, so the screen shows three choices.

Here Player is chosen, so tap Dave. The top line now says paid by Dave, and the wallet icon next to his name marks the payer. Dave is checked under Who Shares for you, with the whole $50, because so far nobody else shares it. Tap him there to take him out if he shouldn't pay a share. Until you pick who paid, the checkmark at the top right stays grey.

Step 2, Who Shares. Dave is already in. Tap the others who share the cost: Jenna, Marcus, Priya and Tyler. With Equally, House Rules splits the amount between everyone checked, Dave included: $50 across five is $10 each. With Manually, tap a player's amount and type their share.

Step 3, Check. Split shows how much of the expense is shared out, $50 of $50, and Left to Split what's left, $0. Then tap the checkmark at the top right of the screen.

You split the whole amount or nobody at all. With part of it split, the checkmark waits and the Check card says what's left. With nobody checked, nobody is charged and the hosts carry the expense.

What changes

The row under Expenses now says Pizza, paid by Dave, $50.

Each share comes off that player's result: Jenna, Marcus, Priya and Tyler are each $10 down. Dave paid $50 and shares $10, so his result goes up by $40. Whatever anyone wins or loses at the table is counted on top.

Until settle-up, the four show under Before You Settle Up as Jenna owes $10, Marcus owes $10 and so on. Together that's $40, the same $40 Dave is owed, and the still owed tile at the top of the cash box shows it. Dave isn't listed: the list shows only who owes. The cash box still holds $600, because no money has changed hands. Nobody has to pay now: these lines can stay unpaid until settlement. At the end of the night, House Rules puts them into the transfers along with everyone's wins and losses (see How Settle Up Works).

Change or delete an expense

  • Tap the expense to open Split Expense again and change who paid or who shares.
  • Swipe left on it for Edit and Delete. Delete asks you to confirm.
  • Touch and hold it for the same choices. An expense the hosts carry alone also has Cover from Rake there, to pay it from the rake instead.
check_circle

An expense that isn't split yet holds up the end of the night: Close Session asks you to split it first. See Close Session Step by Step.

Questions

Should I record what someone buys for themselves? No. Record only what the table shares. A player's own food or drink isn't an expense of the night.

I paid for the pizza from the cash box. Choose Cash Box under Paid From. The cash box goes down by $50, and the players who share it pay their part back at settle-up. If the cash box holds less than the expense, the checkmark waits.

Can the house pay for the pizza? Yes. Choose Rake under Paid From, if there's enough rake, or Hosts and check nobody under Who Shares. Then nobody at the table is charged.

One player didn't eat. Don't check them under Who Shares. The others split the cost between them.

The amount doesn't divide evenly. With Equally, House Rules gives the extra cent to the first players under Who Shares: $10 across three is $3.34, $3.33 and $3.33. Or use Manually and type each share.

Can my club split food the same way every time? Yes, your club's House Rules can set who shares food by default.
