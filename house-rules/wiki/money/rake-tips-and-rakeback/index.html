Money

Rake, Tips and Rakeback

In a home game everyone usually cashes out every chip. In a club, some chips stay on the table at the end: the house's share and the dealer's tips. House Rules calls them:

  • Rake: chips the house takes from the game, to pay for the room, cards and the like.
  • Tips: chips players give the dealer.
  • Rakeback: part of the rake the house gives back to players, usually to regulars or to the night's biggest losers.

You record rake and tips in chips, once everyone has cashed out. The cash box, the session's money (see The Cash Box), shows them in money, at the chip value. A player's result is what they won or lost tonight, and settling up is when House Rules works out who pays whom at the end. If nobody takes rake or tips at your table, you can skip this article.

The example

It's the end of a night at the Maple Street Game.

  • The game: No-Limit Hold'em, 1 chip = $0.25.
  • The chips: six players bought 3,200 chips and cashed out 3,080. So 120 chips are left on the table.
  • Where they go: 80 chips are the rake, $20. 40 chips are tips for Leo, who dealt tonight, $10.
  • Then: Dave, the host, gives the $20 rake back to Marcus and Tyler, the two biggest losers, and pays Leo his $10.

Record rake and tips

When everyone has cashed out but chips are left, the session says Everyone Cashed Out and No chips should be left on the table (now 120). Below, a row Chips Left on the Table asks you to assign them.

  1. Tap Chips Left on the Table. You can also tap the More button (the three dots at the top right) and choose Rake and Tips.
  2. All the leftover chips start in Rake: 120. Type the rake, 80.
  3. Tap the arrow at the top right of the keys. The tips get the rest, 40.
  4. Check that rake and tips add up to On the table, then tap the checkmark that's now in place of the arrow.

Rake and tips must add up to the chips left on the table exactly. That's the point: every chip is accounted for before you settle up.

The session now says Ready to Settle, and a Rake and Tips card shows Rake 80 and Tips 40.

To change them later, tap More, then Rake and Tips.

Rake and tips in the cash box

Open Cash Box and scroll down. There are two cards, both in money at the chip value.

Rake: 80 chips at $0.25 is $20 collected. The other tiles show rakeback you've given, rake spent to expenses (see Expenses), and what's available, the house's money for the night.

Tips: 40 chips is $10 set aside for the dealer, how much you've paid from cash box, and what's left. We'll pay Leo below.

Give rakeback

  1. In the Rake card, tap Distribute Rakeback.
  2. Under Who Gets Rakeback, choose how to share it: Equally, By % or Manually.
  3. Tap the players who get it. With Equally, House Rules shares everything available between them.
  4. Check the Check card: Distributed is what goes to players, Kept by Host is what the house keeps. Then tap the checkmark at the top right.

To keep part of the rake, use Manually and type each player's amount, or By % and give each a share. What you don't give out shows under Kept by Host. You can't give out more than is available.

Back in the cash box, the Rake card lists each player's rakeback, and available is down to $0.

Rakeback is added to a player's result, and they get it at settle-up. Marcus paid $200 for 800 chips and cashed out 236, worth $59: he lost $141. With $10 rakeback he's down $131, and the cash box owes him $69, $59 for his chips plus $10.

Pay the dealer

Tips are money you hold for the dealer. When you hand it over:

  1. In Cash Box, tap Pay Out.
  2. Under Expenses and Tips, tap Dealer Tips. It shows what's left to pay, $10 left.
  3. Choose Cash or Transfer, the way you pay the dealer, then tap the suggestion 10 all tips. It's recorded at once, so there's no checkmark to tap. Above the keys, Left in cash box shows what the cash box holds before you pay.

The toast says Tips paid out, $10. The Tips card now shows $10 paid from cash box and $0 left.

You can't pay out more tips than were set aside.

check_circle

Tips don't count toward anyone's result: they belong to the dealer. Rakeback does: it's money back to the player.

Questions

Do I have to record rake and tips? Only if chips are left on the table. Then yes: House Rules can't settle up while chips are unaccounted for.

The rake and tips don't add up to the chips on the table. They must, to the chip. If a player's cash-out was miscounted, fix the cash-out instead, see The Chips Don't Add Up.

Can I give rakeback before everyone cashes out? Rakeback comes from the rake, and you record rake once everyone has cashed out. So no rake, no rakeback.

Where does the rake money go at the end? What's available after rakeback and expenses is the house's income for the night. It isn't anyone's result, and the club's stats count it as the house's.

Can I pay a pizza from the rake? Yes, see Expenses.
