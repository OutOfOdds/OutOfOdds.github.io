Money

The Cash Box

The cash box is the real money of the session: what players have paid you, the host, for their chips, minus what you've handed back. Chips stay on the table; money goes through the cash box.

You record two kinds of money here:

  • Collect: a player pays you. Most payments are recorded for you when you seat a player or add a rebuy and choose Cash or Transfer on the keys.
  • Pay Out: you hand money to a player, usually when they leave with more chips than they bought.

And one check: Count Cash, to see whether the money in your hands matches what House Rules expects.

The example

It's three hours into a night at the Maple Street Game.

  • The game: No-Limit Hold'em, 1 chip = $0.25, so a 400-chip buy-in costs $100.
  • The money so far: Dave, the host, Marcus, Jenna, Tyler and Priya have bought 2,400 chips. Marcus bought twice. Jenna paid $100 by transfer, everyone else paid cash, $500. So the cash box holds $600.
  • What happens: Priya leaves with 548 chips. At $0.25 a chip they're worth $137, so Dave pays her $137 in cash: the $100 she paid plus her $37 win. Then he counts the cash in his hands.

Open the cash box

In the session, tap Cash Box at the bottom left. The button shows how much it holds.

From top to bottom:

  • In Cash Box: the money you hold for the session, here $600. Under it, in cash and by transfer split it by how it was paid. You see these two once anyone has paid by transfer.
  • collected: everything players have paid you. paid out: everything that left the cash box, to players, for expenses and for dealer tips.
  • still owed: what players haven't paid yet. See When a Player Pays Later.
  • rake: the house's share in money, see Rake, Tips and Rakeback.
  • Before You Settle Up: what's left to do before the end of the night. Here 4 of 5 players are still at the table, which only needs time. Rows with an arrow open where you fix them, like collecting from a player who owes.
  • Expenses: pizza, rent and the like, see Expenses.
  • Operations: every payment and payout, newest first.

The three buttons at the bottom are Collect, Pay Out and Count Cash.

Pay a player out

Priya cashes out first, in the session (see Buy-Ins, Rebuys and Cash-Outs). A cash-out only records her chips. The money is a separate step, here:

  1. In Cash Box, tap Pay Out.
  2. Under Payout Due, tap the player. Players who cashed out and haven't been paid yet are listed here first, with what they're due.
  3. Choose Cash or Transfer above the digits. Cash is chosen to start with.
  4. Tap the suggestion next to the digits, 137 payout due, to pay the whole amount. It's recorded at once. Or type another amount and tap the checkmark at the top right of the keys.

The toast says Money paid out, Priya, $137, with Undo. The cash box now holds $463: $363 in cash and $100 by transfer, and paid out is $137.

You can't pay out more than the cash box holds. If you type more, the keys say so and the checkmark waits.

Collect works the same way, the other direction: tap Collect, pick the player under Owe the Cash Box or Players, enter the amount. The full walk-through is in When a Player Pays Later.

To remember why money moved, tap Note above the digits before you save. The note shows under the operation.

Count the cash

At any point, count the cash in your hands and check it against the cash box. Only cash counts here: transfers went to your bank, not your pocket.

  1. Tap Count Cash at the bottom right.
  2. Type the cash you counted. Above the keys, Should be in cash is what House Rules expects, and Difference is the gap.
  3. Tap the checkmark at the top right of the keys.

Dave counts $343. It should be $363, so the difference is −$20.

When it doesn't match, House Rules shows how much is off, like $20 Short, and what you can do:

  • Find It First: Session Log lists everything that happened tonight, to look for a payment you forgot to record.
  • Leave It for Now: only notes the count. Count again later.
  • Host Covers It: the host makes up the difference. If your club's House Rules name the host, it comes off the host's result. Otherwise it's only marked as covered.
  • Split Among Players: the difference is shared equally by everyone who played tonight, here $4 each, 5 players.

Choose one and tap the checkmark at the top right, or the ✕ at the top left to go back without recording anything.

Dave finds a $20 bill under the chip tray. He taps ✕, then Count Cash again, and types $363. The difference is $0, and the toast says Cash matches.

A count doesn't move any money: In Cash Box stays $463. It's kept in Operations as a Cash Count line with the amount you counted, so you can see when you last counted and whether it matched.

Operations

Scroll down to Operations. Each line is one payment, payout or count: the name, what it was and when, and the amount. Money in has a plus, money out a minus. A Cash Count line shows the amount you counted, not money in. The newest is on top, and the last five show until you tap Show All.

To fix one, swipe it to the left:

  • Edit changes the amount, or the note of a payment or payout. You can also just tap the line.
  • Delete removes it, after you confirm.

Recorded cash that was really a transfer, or the other way round? Touch and hold the line and choose Mark Paid by Transfer or Mark Paid in Cash.

You can't change who a line is for. Delete it and record it again.

check_circle

A finished session's cash box can only be read. To change it, reopen the session from its settlement.

Questions

Why is there money in the cash box I didn't collect by hand? Payments you choose on the buy-in and rebuy keys go straight into the cash box, at the chip value.

A player paid more than they owed. Collect it anyway. The extra goes back to them at settle-up, see How Settle Up Works.

Can the cash box go below zero? No. You can't pay out more than it holds.

Do I have to count the cash? No, it's a check for you. If cash goes missing, it's easier to find the same night.

The cash box doesn't match my pocket and I can't find why. See The Cash Box Doesn't Match.
