Blind Timer

Notifications and Lock Screen

The Blind Timer doesn't need House Rules on screen. When a level ends, your phone gets a notification with the new blinds, even when it's locked or you're in another app. The Lock Screen and the Dynamic Island show the clock too, so a glance at the phone tells you how long the level has left.

This page shows the two settings behind this, what the Lock Screen card and the notification look like, and what to check when something is missing. Starting and running the clock is in Running the Timer.

The example club

All the screenshots come from the Maple Street Game. Dave is the host and also plays, so in this story you are Dave.

  • The game: a tournament at Marcus's place with the built-in Standard template: 8 players, 10,000 chips each, 15-minute levels starting at 50 / 100, a small blind of 50 and a big blind of 100.
  • What happens: Dave starts the clock, puts his phone down next to his chips and plays. When level 1 runs out, the phone tells him the blinds went up.

The two settings

Both are in House Rules' Settings, and both are on from the start. To open Settings, tap the gear at the top right of the Lobby, the app's home screen.

  • Level Change, under Timer: a notification each time the timer moves to the next level on its own.
  • Lock Screen, under Live Activities: a card with the clock on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, the pill-shaped area at the top of newer iPhones. Live Activities are the iPhone's name for such live cards, and this one switch controls both places. The same switch also shows a running cash game there, see Lock Screen and Quick Rebuy.

The other switches on this screen are for club games and debts, not for the timer. Turn either timer switch off if you don't want that part. The timer itself works the same either way.

Start the clock

In the Lobby, tap Blind Timer, tap Standard, then Use Template, and tap Start at the bottom. More on this in Running the Timer. Dave's clock starts at Level 1, 50 / 100.

The first time you start a timer, your phone asks whether House Rules may send notifications. Tap Allow. Without it there are no level alerts, and the timer shows how to turn them on in the iPhone's Settings app, under Apps, House Rules, Notifications.

The card on the Lock Screen

Dave locks his phone. Under the clock on the Lock Screen there's now a card for the timer:

  • The template's name, Standard, and the level, Level 1.
  • The blinds now, 50 / 100, and the time left, 14:42, counting down.
  • A round Pause button.
  • Under them, the next blinds, Next: 75 / 150, and the players left with their average stack in big blinds (BB), the average stack divided by the big blind: 8 of 8 left, average 100 BB, because 10,000 chips is 100 big blinds of 100.

The first time, the iPhone asks under the card: Allow Live Activities from House Rules? Tap Allow to keep the card. With Don't Allow, the card goes away. To turn it back on later, open the iPhone's Settings app, then Apps, House Rules, and turn on Live Activities.

What you can do from the card:

  • Tap Pause to pause the clock without unlocking the phone. The card then says Paused, and the button turns into a play button, a triangle, that resumes it. In the Dynamic Island a paused clock shows a pause sign instead of the time.
  • Tap anywhere else on the card to open the timer in House Rules.

When the level changes

When the 15 minutes of level 1 run out, the timer moves to level 2 by itself, and the phone shows a notification: Level 2, Blinds 75/150. The card above it has moved on too: Level 2, 75 / 150, and the next blinds are 100 / 200. The average is now 66 BB: the same 10,000 chips, but the big blind is 150.

Tap the notification to open the timer.

If you pause the clock, House Rules holds the notifications back, so a paused level doesn't ring when its time would have run out. When you resume, the alerts are set again for the new times.

The Dynamic Island

On an iPhone with the Dynamic Island, the clock moves there when you're in another app or on the Home Screen. On the left it shows the level, L2 for level 2, and on the right the time left, 14:31. Touch and hold it to see the whole card, or tap it to open the timer. An iPhone without the Dynamic Island has a plain black notch or bar at the top: there, the Lock Screen card is where you see the clock.

Back in House Rules, the timer shows the same clock: Level 2, 75 / 150.

When you stop the timer with Stop at the top right, the card and the level alerts go away.

If something is missing

  • No notification when the level changed: check that Level Change is on in House Rules' Settings, and that notifications for House Rules are allowed in the iPhone's Settings app, under Notifications.
  • No card on the Lock Screen: check that Lock Screen is on in House Rules' Settings, and that Live Activities are allowed for House Rules in the iPhone's Settings app.
  • The card went away during a long game: iOS keeps a card for about eight hours at most. Open House Rules and the card comes back.
  • The card says Open to see the level: the timer moved on, but the phone hasn't redrawn the card yet. Just open House Rules, and the card shows the right level again. The notifications still come on time.

Good to know

  • The notification and the card show only the timer: levels, blinds, time and stacks. Money stays in the app.
  • A club tournament's own timer sends the same notifications and has the same card. See Setting Up a Tournament.
  • To keep the clock in view on an iPad, open it in its own window: Timer Window on iPad.
