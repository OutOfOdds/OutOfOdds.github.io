Blind Timer

Timer Window on iPad

On an iPad, the Blind Timer can open in a window of its own: a big clock with nothing else on it, readable from across the room. Stand the iPad at the end of the table, and everyone sees the level, the time left and the blinds coming next. The timer window is only on the iPad: on the iPhone the timer always stays in the app.

This page shows how to open the timer window, what it shows and how to close it. How the timer itself works is in Running the Timer.

The example club

All the screenshots come from the Maple Street Game. Dave is the host and also plays, so in this story you are Dave.

  • The game: eight players, the built-in Standard template: 10,000 chips each and 15-minute levels starting at 50 / 100, a small blind of 50 and a big blind of 100.
  • What happens: Dave starts the clock on his iPad, opens the timer window, and stands the iPad on the sideboard so the whole table can see it.

Start the timer on the iPad

On the iPad, House Rules shows a sidebar on the left. Under Library, tap Blind Timer. The templates open on the right. A template is a ready-made blind schedule: the levels, their blinds and minutes, and the breaks. Built-In has Standard, Turbo and Deep Stack, My Templates has the ones you built, and Build Step by Step at the bottom makes a new one.

Tap Standard. Its levels open, with Use Template at the bottom: tap it. The timer loads: Level 1, 15:00, 50 / 100, Not started. Tap Start, the big button in the middle at the bottom.

On the iPad the buttons at the bottom are bigger and have names: Previous, Pause and Next. At the top right are, from left to right, Open in New Window (an icon of a window with a plus, no label), More (three dots), Stacks, Levels and Stop. Stacks and Levels work as on the iPhone, see Running the Timer.

Open the timer window

Tap Open in New Window, the first icon at the top right. With a keyboard attached, you can also press Shift-Command-T, or swipe down from the top edge of the screen to show the menu bar and choose File, then Open Blind Timer in New Window.

The window shows only the clock, as big as the window allows:

  • Level 1 at the top left, and the template's name, Standard, at the top right with a round close button next to it.
  • The time left, 14:40, across the middle, and the blinds, 50 / 100, under it.
  • The next two levels along the bottom, each with how long until it begins: Level 2, 75 / 150, starts in 14:40, the moment this level ends, and Level 3, 100 / 200, starts in 29:40, one 15-minute level later.
  • Previous, Pause and Next, the same buttons as in the main window.

The clock keeps running between the screenshots on this page, so each one shows a little less time.

Here the window fills the screen. To make it smaller and place it next to House Rules, turn on Windowed Apps in the iPad's Settings, under Multitasking & Gestures. The clock grows or shrinks with the window.

The screen stays on while the timer window is open, so the iPad doesn't go to sleep in the middle of a level. Keep it plugged in for a long tournament.

One clock, two windows

There's only one timer in the app. The window shows the same clock as the main window, and both control it. Dave taps Pause in the timer window when the pizza arrives: the window says Paused, the time stops at 14:33, the Pause button turns into Resume, and the main window is paused too.

He taps Resume, and both windows count down again.

Close the timer window

Tap the round X at the top right of the timer window, next to the template's name. Only the window closes: the timer keeps running, and the main window still shows it.

To stop the clock, tap Stop in the main window. See Running the Timer.

A club tournament's timer

A club tournament you run in House Rules has its own timer, made from the blinds you chose when you set it up. Open the tournament from your club, tap More at the top right and Blind Timer, then Open in New Window. When the timer window is wide enough, for example full screen, it adds a column on the right with the tournament's players left, the average stack, the prize pool and the payouts, so the table sees what they're playing for. See Setting Up a Tournament.

Good to know

  • If the timer window is open but no timer is loaded, it says No Timer Running and asks you to pick a blind structure in the main window.
  • Opening the window again brings the same window forward. There's never more than one.
