Tournaments and Seasons

Counting Only the Best Results

In a long season, some players can't come every time. If every tournament counts, every night adds points, so a player who shows up every time can collect more than a better player who missed a night. Best Results Count fixes that: only each player's highest-scoring tournaments add up. Their lower-scoring ones are dropped, and a missed night just means one fewer result to choose from.

This page also covers ties, sharing the standings, the season's dates, and ending or deleting a season.

The example club

The story picks up where How Season Points Work left off. Dave is the host of the Maple Street Game and also plays, so in this story you are Dave.

  • The season: the 2026 Season, running all of 2026. The club has played five tournaments since late October, all settled and the default points: 10, 7, 5, 3, 2 for the top five places and 1 for playing.
  • The standings: Dave leads with 41 points. He played all five tournaments. Marcus has 37: he won three of the four he played, but missed one night.
  • What happens: the club agrees that missing one night shouldn't cost Marcus the lead. Dave sets Best Results Count to 4, shares the new standings, and looks at the season's dates and how to end it.

Count only the best results

Open your club and tap the season card, here 2026 Season. On the season screen, tap More, the three dots at the top right, then Points Rules. At the bottom, tap Best Results Count and pick a number. All counts every tournament. The other choices are 4, 5, 6, 8, 10 and 12.

Dave picks 4, and the row now reads Best Results Count 4. Tap the checkmark at the top right of Points Rules to save. Nothing changes until you do. The X at the top left closes without saving.

The new standings

The season recounts at once. The headline now says Marcus leads by 2 points, and the line under it adds best 4 count.

Here's why, with the default points plus 1 for playing:

  • Marcus played only four tournaments, so all four still count: 11 + 11 + 11 for his three wins (10 plus 1 each) and 4 for a 4th place (3 plus 1). That's 37, the same as before.
  • Dave played five. His results were 11 for his win, 8 for each of his three 2nd places (7 plus 1), and 6 for his 3rd place (5 plus 1), 41 in all. The best four are 11 + 8 + 8 + 8 = 35. His weakest result, the 6, no longer counts.
  • Jenna also loses her weakest result, a 4 for 4th place: 33 becomes 29.
  • Tyler, Priya, Sam and Kevin played five too, so each loses their lowest result. Rosa played four and keeps all of them: 4 points either way.

Played still shows every tournament a player played. Only the points change. The Tournaments list below is the same, all five still belong to the season.

Pick a number a few below how many tournaments you expect in the season. If you play about twelve, 8 or 10 lets each player skip two to four nights. Dave picks 4 only because his season has five tournaments so far. He can raise it later, and the season recounts.

Ties

When two players have the same points:

  1. The one with more wins goes first.
  2. If wins are equal too, House Rules looks at each player's own latest tournament. The one who finished higher in theirs goes first.

When the top two are tied on points, the headline says who leads on wins instead of a number of points.

Share the standings

Tap the share button at the top right of the season screen, the square with an arrow. Your phone's share sheet opens with 2026 Season, so you can send it to the club's group chat, a note or any other app.

The text has the season's name, the club, the dates and each place with points, like 1. Marcus, 37 pts. It has no money.

Season dates

On the season screen, tap More and Season Dates.

  • Starts: the first day of the season. Here Jan 1, 2026.
  • Ends: turn it off for a season with no last day. Turn it on to pick the Last Day, here Dec 31, 2026.

The note sums up the rule: Club tournaments that start in these dates and reach settlement score points. Tap the checkmark to save, or the X to close without changes. A shorter league, like a fall league from September to November, is just a season with those dates.

To change the name, tap More and Rename Season.

End a season

When the league is over, tap More and End Season. House Rules asks first: End Season Today? Tournaments after today won't score points in this season. Tap End Season to confirm. There's no Cancel button: tap outside the box to cancel and keep the season running.

Ending a season sets today as its Last Day. Tournaments that start today still count, those that start later don't. The standings stay as they are, and you can still open the season. End Season is only in the menu while the season is running. Dave keeps his running.

To start the next season, open the club, tap More and New Season, as in How Season Points Work.

Delete a season

More and Delete Season removes the season. House Rules asks first: Delete Season? Tournaments and their results stay. Only the season's standings go.

Nothing is lost for good: the standings are counted from the tournaments, so a new season with the same dates and rules shows the same table again.

Good to know

  • Best Results Count works with both By Place and By Field. See How Season Points Work.
  • A player who played fewer tournaments than the count keeps all their results.
  • Changing Best Results Count, like any change in Points Rules, recounts the whole season at once.
