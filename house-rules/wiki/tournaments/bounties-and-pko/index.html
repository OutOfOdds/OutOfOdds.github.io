Tournaments and Seasons

Fixed Bounties and PKO

In a bounty tournament every player has a price on their head. Each entry pays a bounty on top of the buy-in, and whoever knocks that player out collects it. It gives everyone a reason to play for knockouts, not only for the top places.

House Rules has two kinds:

  • Fixed: the player who knocks someone out gets the whole bounty in cash.
  • Progressive, also called PKO (progressive knockout): they get half of that player's bounty in cash, and the other half is added to their own bounty. The more players you knock out, the bigger your own bounty grows, and the more you're worth to whoever knocks you out.

Bounties are separate from the prize pool. The places share the buy-ins, the knockers share the bounties, and House Rules keeps track of both and pays them together at the end.

The example tournament

All the screenshots come from the Maple Street Game's first PKO. Dave is the host and also plays. He runs the tournament on his phone, so in this story you are Dave.

  • The price: a $40 buy-in, a $20 progressive bounty and a $5 house fee: $65 an entry. Re-entry is open until Level 4: a player who goes out can pay $65 again for a new stack and a new bounty.
  • The players: eight, all paid. Eight entries of $65 bring $520 into the cash box: $320 for the prize pool, $160 for bounties and $40 of house fees.
  • What happens: Jenna knocks out Sam. Marcus knocks out Rosa, who re-enters. Tyler and Priya knock out Kevin together. Then the rest go out one by one, and Jenna wins.

Turn on bounties

You set bounties when you create the tournament. In New Tournament, next to the buy-in:

  1. Set Bounty to Fixed or Progressive. Dave picks Progressive.
  2. Set the Bounty Amount, here $20.

Each entry then pays the buy-in, the bounty and the house fee. The rest of New Tournament is in Setting Up a Tournament.

The tournament with bounties

The Prize Pool card now also shows the bounties: 8 entries, $160 in bounties, $40 house fee. One of the tiles counts the bounties paid out so far, $0 of $160 bounties won.

In Still In, the list of players who are still playing, each row shows the player's current bounty next to a target symbol, $20 for everyone at the start.

Knock someone out

Jenna wins a big pot and Sam is out. Dave taps Player Out in the bar at the bottom of the tournament screen. In the sheet that opens he taps Player and picks Sam, then taps the Knocked Out By row and picks Jenna. How busts work in general is in When a Player Is Out.

In a bounty tournament you must pick who knocked the player out, because the bounty goes to them. Until you do, you can't save.

Under the knocker, House Rules spells out the bounty: Jenna gets $10. Half of Sam’s $20 bounty. Jenna’s bounty grows to $30.

Tap the checkmark at the top right of the sheet to save. A message at the bottom of the screen says Sam out in 8th and Jenna +$10, with an Undo button. The tile now reads $10 of $160 bounties won.

With a Fixed bounty, Jenna would get all $20 in cash, and her own bounty would stay $20.

A re-entry brings a new bounty

Marcus knocks out Rosa. Re-entry is still open, so Rosa buys back in. In the Player Out sheet Dave picks Rosa, taps Re-Enter instead of Out, and picks Marcus under Knocked Out By.

Marcus still collects Rosa's bounty: Marcus gets $10. Half of Rosa’s $20 bounty. Marcus’s bounty grows to $30. Rosa pays $65 again, and the note says what she gets: New stack of 20,000 and a new $20 bounty, $65 to the cash box.

Rosa's second $65 adds $40 to the prize pool, $20 to the bounties and $5 to the house fee. The pool is now $360 and the bounties $180.

Two players knock someone out

Kevin goes all in against Tyler and Priya and loses to both: each of them wins part of the pot. They split his bounty. Under Knocked Out By, pick both players. You can pick up to two. A third tap replaces the second.

The split is even: Tyler gets $5 and Priya gets $5. Half of Kevin’s $20 bounty, split. Tyler’s bounty grows to $25. Priya’s bounty grows to $25.

When an amount can't be split evenly to the dollar, the first player you picked gets the extra dollar.

Follow the bounties

After three knockouts the Prize Pool card shows 9 entries, $180 in bounties, $45 house fee and $30 of $180 bounties won.

In Still In, each player's row says how many players they've knocked out and how much they've won, and the bounty on their head now: Jenna 1 knockout, won $10, with a $30 bounty. Rosa's row says 2 entries: she's on her second stack, with a new $20 bounty.

Halves that don't split to the dollar

Later Marcus knocks out Tyler, whose bounty is $25. Half of $25 is $12.50. House Rules pays the cash part in whole dollars, rounded down, and adds the rest to the knocker's bounty: Marcus gets $12 in cash, and the other $13 is added to his bounty, which grows from $30 to $43.

The winner keeps their own bounty

Dave, Tyler, Priya and Rosa go out. Jenna knocks out Dave, Priya and Rosa, Marcus knocks out Tyler. At the end it's Jenna against Marcus, and Jenna wins: Jenna gets $21. Half of Marcus’s $43 bounty. Jenna’s bounty grows to $85. The line at the bottom says Marcus's prize for 2nd: Wins $108.

Nobody knocks out the winner, so Jenna keeps the $85 on her own head. That's why progressive bounties reward the winner twice: 1st place, and the biggest bounty in the room.

The Tournament Over card says Jenna wins $180, plus $148 in bounties. Her $148 is the $63 she collected in cash plus her own $85 bounty. The cash came from five knockouts: Sam $10, Dave $10, Priya $12 (half of $25), Rosa $10 and Marcus $21.

Final Standings shows each player's prize, bounties and what they paid. Rosa paid $130 for two entries, so her $72 for 3rd still leaves her −$58. Priya and Tyler each won $5 in bounties.

Settle up

Tap Settle Up. From Cash Box pays each player their prize and bounties in one amount: Jenna $328 ($180 prize and $148 bounties), Marcus $130 ($108 and $22), Rosa $72, Tyler $5 and Priya $5.

Check the box: nine entries of $65 brought in $585. Out go $360 in prizes and $180 in bounties, $540 in all. What's left, $45, is the house fee. The closing steps are in Close Session, Step by Step.

Mistakes

  • Wrong knocker, right after saving: tap Undo in the message, then record the bust again. The bounty goes back to where it was.
  • Wrong knocker, later: only the latest bust can be undone. Scroll down to the Out list under Still In, hold the last player's row and choose Back In, then fix the busts in order. See When a Player Is Out.

Good to know

  • Bounties aren't part of the prize pool, and the guarantee and dealer tips don't touch them.
  • A deal doesn't touch bounties either. Each player keeps the bounties they've won, and players in the deal keep their own heads. See Making a Deal.
  • Every re-entry brings a new bounty. The grown bounty from the player's earlier stack went to whoever knocked them out.
  • Season points can count knockouts. See Season Points.
