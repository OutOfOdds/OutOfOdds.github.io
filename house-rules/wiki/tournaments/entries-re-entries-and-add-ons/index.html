Tournaments and Seasons

Entries, Re-Entries and Add-Ons

During a tournament, every time a player pays to play you record it in House Rules:

  • an entry puts a player in the tournament with the starting stack;
  • a re-entry brings back a player who busted, with a fresh stack, for the same price as an entry;
  • an add-on gives a player who is still in one extra stack, at its own price.

From these House Rules counts the prize pool, the chips in play, the places paid and the money in your cash box. Every entry and re-entry pays the full price, buy-in plus house fee. An add-on pays its own price, and all of it goes to the prize pool.

The example tournament

All the screenshots come from one tournament at the Maple Street Game. Dave, the host, records everything on his phone, so in this story you are Dave.

  • The price: a $50 buy-in plus a $5 house fee, $55 an entry, for 20,000 chips. Re-entry is open until Level 4. The add-on is $20 for 10,000 chips. How these are set is in Setting Up a Tournament.
  • Twenty minutes in: Dave, Jenna, Marcus, Priya, Sam and Tyler are in. Everyone has paid except Sam, who'll pay later. That's a $300 prize pool and $275 in the cash box.
  • What happens next, in this order: Kevin arrives late. Then Rosa, a friend of Jenna's who has never played at the club. Tyler busts and re-enters. Later Jenna, Marcus and Dave take the add-on.

The bar at the bottom of the tournament has three buttons: the cash box with the money you hold, Player Out, and +. Scroll down and you find Still In, the list of players who are still playing, one row each.

Add a player

Tap + and choose Add Player.

The Add Player sheet opens. At the top there's a search field, then the Paid switch, New Member and the Club Members who aren't in the tournament yet.

Paid or not

Before you tap a name, check Paid:

  • On when the player has paid you. The note under it says what goes to the cash box now, $55 here.
  • Off when they'll pay later. The player is in the tournament anyway and owes the entry until they pay you in the cash box. See The Cash Box.

Sam played on credit like this: his row in Still In says owes $55.

A club member

Tap their name. That's the whole entry: the player is in, with the starting stack, and a short message slides up at the bottom of the screen, Kevin is in, $55 paid.

The sheet stays open, so you can add several players in a row. Tap the cross at the top right when you're done.

Tapped the wrong name?

Tap Undo in the message right away. The entry goes, and so does its payment in the cash box. Kevin is back in the list.

The message stays only a few seconds. If it's gone, see Mistakes below.

Someone new

Rosa has never played at the club, so she isn't in the list. Dave types her name in the search field and taps Add “Rosa”. She becomes a club member and is in the tournament at once, paid, like any other entry. You can also tap New Member and type the name there.

Back in the tournament, the two late entries are counted: 8 entries, a $400 prize pool and $40 of house fees. The places paid follow the entries when payouts are on Auto, the default: 2 places up to 7 entries, 3 places from 8. See Prize Pool and Payouts.

A re-entry

Tyler loses all his chips early. Re-entry is open until Level 4, so he pays again and comes back.

  1. Tap Player Out.
  2. Tap the Player row at the top of the sheet and pick Tyler from the list.
  3. Choose Re-Enter instead of Out in 8th.
  4. Check Paid, the same as for an entry.
  5. Tap the checkmark at the top right of the sheet.

Tyler stays in the tournament with a new 20,000 stack, and his row now shows 2 entries. The prize pool grows by $50 and the cash box by $55.

A note under Re-Enter and Out in 8th tells you when re-entry closes, Re-entry closes after Level 4 here. After that, the sheet has no Re-Enter: a busted player is out.

When re-entry closes

  • You choose how long re-entry stays open when you set up the tournament: until a blind level or until a break. See Setting Up a Tournament.
  • It closes by the tournament's own blind timer. If the timer isn't running, re-entry stays open, and whether a player may still re-enter is up to you.
  • Re-entry is for a player who just busted. Marking players out is in When a Player Is Out.

The add-on

Each player who is still in can take one add-on. There are two ways to record it.

From the + menu

Good for the add-on break, when several players take it at once.

  1. Tap + and choose Add-On.
  2. Check Paid.
  3. Tap each player who takes it.

Each tap records the add-on at once and shows a message, like Add-on for Marcus, $20 paid, with Undo. The player leaves the list, since it's one add-on per player.

From a player's row

Good for one player. Touch and hold the player's row in Still In and choose Add-On. This records a paid add-on right away, with the same message and Undo. For an add-on that isn't paid yet, use the + menu with Paid off.

Players who took the add-on say add-on under their name.

What's in the cash box

Tap the cash box button at the bottom left. Dave holds $500: eight paid entries at $55, Tyler's re-entry among them, plus three add-ons at $20. Sam's $55 is still owed. The rake tile is the house fee on all 9 entries, $45, Sam's $5 included. Everything else in the box belongs to the prize pool.

Sam can pay any time before the end. When he does, tap Collect at the bottom of the cash box. See The Cash Box.

Mistakes

  • Right after an entry or add-on: tap Undo in the message. It removes the payment from the cash box too.
  • A payment recorded by mistake, after the message is gone: open the cash box, find it under Operations, swipe it left, tap Delete and confirm.
  • A player you added by mistake: hold their row and choose Delete Player. The item appears only once the player has nothing in the cash box, so delete their payment first.
  • A wrong bust or re-entry: see When a Player Is Out.

Good to know

  • Every entry and re-entry pays the full price, buy-in plus house fee. The add-on pays no house fee.
  • One add-on per player, only for players still in.
  • If House Rules draws the seats, a late entry gets a seat at the shortest table. See Tables and Seats.
  • Auto pays more places as entries grow. Re-entries count as entries.
