Tournaments and Seasons

Tables and Seats

When a tournament has more players than one table can hold, House Rules can seat everyone for you. It draws a random seat for each player, tells you when the tables get uneven and when to close one, and finally draws the final table. You read the seats off your phone and say them out loud.

You don't have to use this. If your game fits at one table, or you prefer to seat people yourself, leave it off and nothing on this page shows up.

The example tournament

All the screenshots come from one night at the Maple Street Game. Dave, the host, runs the tournament on his phone, so in this story you are Dave.

  • The price: a $50 buy-in plus a $5 house fee, $55 an entry, 20,000 chips, no re-entry. How these are set is in Setting Up a Tournament.
  • The players: fifteen, all paid. That's a $750 prize pool and $825 in the cash box.
  • The room: Dave's basement has three tables of 6 seats.
  • What happens: Dave draws the seats, moves one player by hand, then follows the app's hints as players go out, until the last six sit at the final table.

Turn on seat drawing

You switch it on when you create the tournament. In New Tournament, under Tables:

  1. Turn on Draw Seats.
  2. Choose Seats per Table, from 2 to 10. Dave picks 6.
  3. Choose Tables. With Auto, House Rules counts the players when you draw and opens as many tables as they need. Or pick a number from 1 to 8.

This is the only place to turn it on. Once the tournament has started, you can't add seat drawing to it.

The rest of New Tournament is in Setting Up a Tournament.

Draw the seats

Wait until the players are in. On the tournament screen, scroll down below Payouts to the Tables card. Until you draw, it says Seats aren’t drawn yet and has one button, Draw Seats.

Tap Draw Seats. House Rules asks first and says what will happen: 15 players get random seats at 3 tables of 6. Late entries go to the shortest table. Tap Draw Seats again to confirm.

The players are split as evenly as possible. Fifteen players at three tables of 6 means 5 at each table. Seats inside a table are random.

The Tables card now lists each table with how many seats are taken, 5 of 6. And in Still In, the list of players who are still playing, each player's row shows their table and seat under the name, like Table 2, Seat 5.

A player who joins after the draw, with a late entry (someone who arrives after the start) or a re-entry, gets a random free seat at the table with the fewest players. See Entries, Re-Entries and Add-Ons.

Show the seats to the players

The fastest way to seat a room is to let everyone read their seat themselves. Tap the Tables card to open the Tables screen, then tap the Seating button at the top right, the one with a display icon, next to More.

Seating fills the whole screen with big, easy-to-read numbers: each name with its table and seat. A switch at the top has two views: Names lists everyone in alphabetical order, Tables shows them table by table. Put the phone on the table or hold it up, and close it with X when you're done.

The Tables screen

You can also reach the Tables screen from More (the three dots at the top right of the tournament) and Tables.

It shows each table with its seats from 1 up. A seat with nobody in it says Open. A small D marks the seat with the dealer button, so the dealer at each table knows where to start. House Rules only shows the button, it doesn't deal or move it for you. Under the table's name you see how many seats are taken and where the button is, like 5 of 6 seats, button on 1.

List and Map at the top switch between two views of the same seats. Map draws each table as an oval with the players around it, the way they actually sit.

House Rules remembers which view you used last.

Move a player

Before the first hand, Tyler asks to move away from the air vent. Dave taps Tyler's row. The Move Tyler sheet opens:

A switch at the top of the sheet has two choices:

  • Open Seat lists the free seats at every table. Tap one and tap the checkmark at the top right.
  • Swap With lists the other players. Pick one, tap the checkmark, and the two trade seats.

The line under the switch says where the player sits now, Now at Table 1, Seat 1.

If you hold a player's row instead of tapping it, a menu opens with Move to Seat, Swap With and Player Out. Move to Seat opens the same sheet as a tap.

The dealer button doesn't move with the player. After the move, Tyler's old seat 1 is open and still has the button. To move the button, hold any seat and choose Button Here.

More on the Tables screen

More at the top right of the Tables screen has:

  • Add Table opens one more empty table, when a second room or a spare table frees up.
  • Break Table 3 closes the table with the highest number. Its players move to the shortest tables. It asks first. You can't pick another table to break. To keep Table 3 and close a different one, move its players yourself.
  • Seats per Table changes how many seats each table has. You can't choose fewer seats than someone is already sitting in: Seats per Table 5 fails while a player sits in seat 6.
  • Draw Seats Again gives everyone still in a new random seat. It asks first.
  • Balance Tables appears only when the tables are uneven, and makes the move House Rules suggests, the same move as the hint below.

Follow the hints as players go out

You record busts as usual, with Player Out. See When a Player Is Out. After each bust House Rules checks the tables. When it's time to change something, a hint appears at the top of the tournament screen, above the prize pool. Tapping the hint opens a small panel with a button. Tap that button to do what the hint says. To leave things as they are, tap anywhere else.

There are three hints. If more than one applies, House Rules shows the most useful one: the final table first, then breaking a table, then balancing.

Break a table

Chris, Hannah and Ben go out. Twelve players are left: 4 at Table 3 and 8 at Tables 1 and 2. Tables 1 and 2 have 12 seats, so the 4 free seats there are enough for Table 3's players. The hint says Break Table 3: The other tables have room for the 4 players of Table 3.

Dave taps the hint, then Break Table 3. Each of Table 3's players gets a random free seat at the table with the fewest players. A message confirms how many players moved. Their new seats show in Still In and on the Tables screen, so Dave reads them out.

The app always breaks the table with the highest number.

Balance the tables

When one table has two or more players more than another, House Rules suggests moving one player from the fullest table to an open seat at the shortest one. It picks the player at random, and keeps the same suggestion until the seats change.

Grace, Omar, Nina and Leo go out. Eight are left at two tables: 5 at Table 2 and 3 at Table 1. The hint says Balance the Tables: Move Priya from Table 2 to Table 1, Seat 2.

Tap the hint. Move Priya makes the move. Choose Another Player opens the Tables screen, where you tap the row of the player you'd rather move.

Once the tables are even, the hint goes away.

Draw the final table

Rosa and Kevin go out. Six players are left, and one table of 6 holds them all. The hint says Final Table: 6 players left. Everyone draws a new seat at the final table.

Tap the hint, then Draw Final Table. The other tables close and the last six get new random seats at Table 1.

From here on there are no more table hints. The tournament goes on as usual until one player is left.

Mistakes

  • A player moved to the wrong seat: move them again from the Tables screen. Moves don't touch money or places.
  • The draw came out badly, for example two rivals side by side: More, Draw Seats Again on the Tables screen. Everyone still in gets a new random seat.
  • You'd rather not follow a hint: you don't have to. Ignore it, or move players yourself on the Tables screen. The hint stays until the tables are fixed.

Good to know

  • Seats change only when you draw, move, balance or break a table. Nobody moves on their own.
  • Busted players lose their seat. Their seat shows as Open.
  • If you set Tables to a number in New Tournament, the draw opens that many tables even if fewer would do.
  • Seat drawing works with re-entry, add-ons and bounties the same way.
