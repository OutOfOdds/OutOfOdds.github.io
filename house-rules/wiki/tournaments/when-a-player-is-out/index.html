Tournaments and Seasons

When a Player Is Out

When a player loses all their chips, they are out, or "bust". You record it in House Rules with Player Out. The app gives them their finishing place, updates the players left and the average stack, and when only one player is left, it ends the tournament and names the winner.

The example tournament

All the screenshots come from one tournament at the Maple Street Game. Dave, the host, records everything on his phone, so in this story you are Dave. Dave also plays, as many hosts do.

  • The price: a $50 buy-in plus a $5 house fee, $55 an entry, for 20,000 chips. Re-entry is open until Level 4. How these are set is in Setting Up a Tournament.
  • The players: Dave, Jenna, Marcus, Priya, Sam and Tyler. Everyone has paid except Sam, who owes $55. The prize pool is the six $50 buy-ins, $300, whether paid yet or not. The $5 house fees stay with the house. Auto, the default payout plan, pays two places for six players: $195 for 1st and $105 for 2nd.
  • What happens: Priya goes out to Jenna. Dave records it, undoes it, and records it again. Then Sam, Dave and Tyler go out, and finally Marcus, so Jenna wins. At the end Dave tries Back In to see how a mistake is fixed.

Record a bust

There are two ways to start:

  • Tap Player Out in the bar at the bottom of the tournament screen, between the cash box and +. Then tap Player and choose who went out.
  • Or scroll down to Still In, the list of players who are still playing, and tap the player's row. The Player Out sheet opens with that player already chosen.

4 more out to the money under Payouts means four more players must go out before everyone left gets a prize.

Priya loses a big hand to Jenna. Dave taps Priya's row.

The sheet has three parts:

  1. Player: who went out, here Priya.
  2. Re-Enter or Out in 6th. Tap one to choose it, and a check appears next to it. Out is chosen at the start. While re-entry is open, the player can buy back in instead of leaving. The note under it says when re-entry closes, Re-entry closes after Level 4. Out shows the place they finish in. With six players, the first one out is 6th. Re-entry is in Entries, Re-Entries and Add-Ons.
  3. Knocked Out By: who won their last chips. It starts at Nobody. Tap it and pick a player if you want to keep track of knockouts. In a tournament without bounties it's optional.

The line at the bottom says what the player gets: No prize, 5 players left.

Tap the checkmark button at the top right to save. A message at the bottom confirms it, Priya out in 6th.

Undo a bust

Picked the wrong player? Tap Undo in the message while it's still there. The player is back in Still In with their place cleared, as if nothing happened.

Dave records Priya's bust again, this time for real. Under Still In a new list appears, Out: each player who went out, with the time, who knocked them out, and their place.

Once the message is gone, you can still undo the latest bust. Hold that player's row in Out and choose Back In. It does the same as Undo: the player goes back to Still In and their place is cleared.

Only the latest bust can be undone. If Priya went out, then Sam, you bring Sam back first, then Priya.

Nobody knocked them out

Sam runs out of chips in a hand with three players, and nobody is sure whose chips finished him. Dave leaves Knocked Out By at Nobody and saves. Five players were left, so Sam finishes 5th and four remain.

Sam still owes his $55. Going out doesn't change that. He pays before the end, and Dave records it in the cash box. See The Cash Box.

The last bust ends the tournament

Dave goes out 4th to Marcus, Tyler 3rd to Jenna. Two players are left, Jenna and Marcus, and both are in the money. From now on a Deal button also shows in the bottom bar, for when the last players agree to split the prize pool instead of playing on. See Making a Deal.

Marcus goes all in and loses to Jenna. Dave records Marcus out in 2nd, knocked out by Jenna. The line at the bottom of the sheet now says what Marcus gets: Wins $105.

Save, and the tournament is over. The message says Marcus out in 2nd and Jenna wins. If the blind timer runs for this tournament, it pauses at the last bust.

The tournament screen turns into Tournament Over:

  • Ready to Settle is a card, not a button. It names the winner and the prize, Jenna wins $195, with the number of players, entries and the prize pool.
  • Final Standings lists everyone by place, with what they won and paid. The number on the right is their result for the night: Jenna +$140 ($195 won minus $55 paid), Marcus +$50, everyone else −$55.
  • Settle Up at the bottom opens the closing steps: you pay the prizes out of the cash box and record it, collect what's still owed, like Sam's $55, and close the tournament. The app records the money, it doesn't move it. See Prize Pool and Payouts.

Undo the last bust after the end

Say Dave recorded the wrong player as the last one out. Before Settle Up, hold Marcus's row in Final Standings and choose Back In.

Marcus goes back to Still In next to Jenna, and the tournament is running again with 2 of 6 players left. Jenna isn't the winner anymore until the next bust. Now record the bust that really happened.

Good to know

  • Places go down from the bottom: with six players, the first out is 6th, the next 5th, and so on. You never type a place.
  • A player who re-enters doesn't get a place. They are back in with a new stack. See Entries, Re-Entries and Add-Ons.
  • Knocked Out By counts knockouts in the club's statistics and in season points, if your season gives points for knockouts. See Season Points.
  • In a bounty tournament you must pick who knocked the player out, up to two players, because the bounty goes to them. See Fixed Bounties and PKO.
  • Going out doesn't touch money. Unpaid entries stay owed until the player pays.
