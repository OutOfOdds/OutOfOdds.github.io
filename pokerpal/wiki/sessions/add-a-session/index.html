Sessions

Add a session

Log a session you've already played. You answer five short questions, and PokerPal works out your result. Usually, the only numbers you type are what you put in and what you took out.

This guide follows one example evening: a $1/$3 cash game at Riverside Poker Room, $300 in and $742 out.

Still playing? Use a live session instead. It keeps the clock and your buy-ins as you go.

Start a new session

  1. Open the Sessions tab.
  2. Tap New Session.
  3. Tap Log Session.

The screen that opens shows a short receipt at the top, with one line per question, and a panel at the bottom for the question you're on. The panel moves to the next question each time you answer. To change an answer, tap its line on the receipt.

Step 1: Location

Pick where you played. Your places are listed with the most recent first.

If you haven't saved any places yet, you'll see two general ones: Live Game and Online. Pick one of them, or add your own:

  1. Tap New Place.
  2. Type the name.
  3. Choose Live Game or Online.
  4. Check the Currency. A place remembers its currency, so future sessions there use it automatically.
  5. Tap ✓.

Step 2: Game

Choose what you played:

  • Format: Cash, Tournament or SNG (a sit-and-go, a small tournament that starts when the seats fill).
  • Game: NLHE (No-Limit Hold'em), PLO (Pot-Limit Omaha) and its five-card, six-card and Hi-Lo versions, Short Deck or Mixed. Tap Custom to add your own.
  • SB and BB: the small and big blind. BB fills in at twice SB until you set it yourself, so for $1/$3 you enter both. Ante, the small bet everyone posts before a hand, is optional.
  • Limit: No Limit unless you change it.
  • Table: the number of seats, from 2 to 10. You can leave it unset.

Blinds are worth filling in. They let PokerPal suggest buy-ins in the next step and show your results in big blinds (BB), the usual way to compare results across stakes. At $1/$3, one big blind is $3. For tournament fields, see Cash games and tournaments.

Step 3: Invested

Enter everything you put on the table: your first buy-in plus any rebuys, as one total. If you bought in for $300 and reloaded for $200, enter $500.

Once the big blind is set, the keypad offers 100 BB and 200 BB as one-tap amounts.

Step 4: Received

Enter the total you walked away with. The panel shows your result as you type.

Two keys cover the common endings:

  • Break Even enters the amount you invested.
  • Busted enters 0. In a tournament, this key reads No Prize.

Step 5: When

The session is dated today unless you change it. Tap the left arrow to go back a day, then choose how long you played:

  • Tap a length from 2 h to 8 h, and fine-tune it with −15 min and +15 min.
  • Tap Not Known if you don't remember. The session is still saved, and you can add the time later.
  • Tap Exact Time to enter when you started and finished, plus any breaks.

Check and save

After the last step, you'll see your Net Result and the full receipt. Tap any line to change it.

Before you save, you can fill in Details. This section is optional and holds:

  • Tips and Expenses
  • Hands Count
  • Notes

Online cash games show Tips only if you've already entered some. Tournaments have their own details, such as your finishing place and the field size.

Your result is Received minus Invested, minus any tips and expenses that aren't already taken out of what you received. In this example, that's $742 − $300 = $442.

When you've entered at least 15 minutes of play, the summary also shows your hourly rate, and in a cash game with blinds set, your big blinds per hour.

Tap ✓ at the top right to save. The ✓ stays dimmed until Invested and Received are filled in. Your session then appears at the top of the Sessions list.

Log a game you play often

Once you've played two or more different games, Log Session opens with your Recent Games first. PokerPal lists up to five, newest first.

Tap a game to fill in everything except the result and the time, then enter what you received. Tap New Game at the bottom to start from the receipt instead.

If you skip this list, a new session still starts from your last completed one, with the same place, game and blinds. You only fill in the money and the time.

Leave without saving

Tap ✕ at the top left. If you've changed anything, PokerPal asks before throwing it away. Tap Discard to leave.

Questions

Can I change a session after saving it?

Yes. Open it from the Sessions list. See Edit and delete a session.

Why is the ✓ dimmed?

Invested or Received is still empty. A cash game needs an Invested amount above 0. A tournament can be a freeroll, which means it's free to enter.

I don't remember how long I played. Does that affect my stats?

Your result counts as usual. The session just has no hourly rate, and PokerPal never assumes a length for you.

What if saving fails?

Your answers stay on the screen. Tap ✓ again, and you won't get a duplicate session.
