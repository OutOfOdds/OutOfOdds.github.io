A score keeper for Skyjo game nights. Enter each player’s round total, or their cards on a grid, and the app keeps the score. Scoring works offline and needs no account.

How to use it

  1. Start a new game and add 2–8 players.
  2. After each round, enter every player’s total. Prefer the cards? Enter them on the grid and matching columns are discarded automatically.
  3. The app applies the doubling for the player who closed the round and keeps the running total.
  4. When someone reaches the target, the final screen shows the results.

Free and Pro

Scoring, card entry, the score table and the final screen are free. The first 3 games have no limit, then you can start one new game a day. Your games list shows the last 5 finished games.

Skyjo Scorepad Pro is an optional one-time purchase, not a subscription. It unlocks unlimited games, your full game history, full stats and house rules.

Restore a purchase

Connect to the internet, use the Apple Account that made the purchase, open Settings and choose Restore purchase. If Pro is not restored, check the connection and the account, then try again.

iCloud sync

Sync is free and on by default. Your games and players are kept in your own iCloud account, so they appear on your other devices signed in to that account. You can turn sync off in Settings; the change applies the next time the app starts.
