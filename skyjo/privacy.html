Privacy Policy

Updated 4 November 2026

Skyjo Scorepad does not collect your data. This policy explains what stays on your device, what Apple handles and how this support website works.

Who is responsible

Skyjo Scorepad is provided by Mike Mailyan, Russia. For privacy questions, contact support@oddlyworking.app.

Information on your device

No account is required. Player names, scores, game history, house rules and app settings are stored on your device. The app has no developer-operated server, so none of this is sent to the developer.

Apple device backups may include local app data, depending on your backup settings.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, the app keeps your games and players in the private database of your own iCloud account so they can appear on your other devices. Sync is on by default and can be turned off in Settings. The developer cannot read this data. Apple handles it under its own privacy policy.

Purchases and ratings

Apple processes Pro purchases and restoration. The app uses StoreKit transaction information to check whether Pro is active. The app does not receive your payment card details.

The request to rate the app is shown by iOS. Ratings and reviews go to Apple, not through the app.

No analytics, ads or tracking

The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party SDKs. It does not track you and does not request your location, contacts or advertising identifier.

Support correspondence

If you email support, the sender address, message and any attachments you choose to send are handled to respond to your request. Our email service provider also handles correspondence. Avoid sending passwords, payment details or information that is not needed for support.

Retention and your choices

Information on your device stays until you change or delete it, or remove the app. Data in iCloud is managed through your Apple Account settings. Support correspondence is kept for handling the request and necessary follow-up or legal obligations. You may ask about access, correction or deletion by emailing support; available rights depend on applicable law.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. Player names are whatever you type and stay on your device and in your iCloud account.

This website

This static support website is hosted by GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics scripts, advertising pixels, contact forms or cookies to its pages. The hosting service may process IP addresses and technical request information under GitHub’s privacy statement.

Policy updates

Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to support@oddlyworking.app.
